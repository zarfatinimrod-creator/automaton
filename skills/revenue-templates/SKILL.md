# Templates — director playbook

Channels: Etsy (Etsy Payments supports Israel through Payoneer; PayPal is not available to Israeli sellers; 4.5% + ₪2 processing plus listing/transaction fees) and an own store via the merchant-of-record checkout. Gumroad payouts for Israel are unconfirmed — do not rely on it.

Etsy rules (2026): AI-assisted digital products are allowed with disclosure and must be designed by the seller; prompt packs and templated resale are banned and enforced. Every listing: original design, walkthrough PDF, "make a copy" link for Sheets/Notion plus an .xlsx.

Loop
1. Pick a task people search for (Hebrew freelancer bookkeeping, Israeli VAT/osek patur tracker, rental tracker, event budget in ILS, CRM/inventory, Hebrew planners, Jewish-holiday printables).
2. Build, test with sample data, screenshot, write the listing (title with search terms, 5+ images, price ₪25-90 / $6-25).
3. Publish; record views and sales daily with revenue_kpi; sales via revenue_record source=etsy external_id=<receipt id>.
4. After 30 listings, double down on the category with the best views-to-sales ratio; retire listings with zero views after 60 days.

Median reality: Etsy digital shops earn a few hundred pounds a month at the median; power law applies. Volume of good listings + SEO is the lever.
