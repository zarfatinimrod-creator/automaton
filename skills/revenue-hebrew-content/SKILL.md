# Hebrew content — director playbook

2026 reality: Google's spam updates penalise scaled AI content and thin affiliate sites; AI Overviews cut click-through sharply. What still earns: first-hand tool tests with published data, continuously refreshed calculators/datasets, and Hebrew coverage of Israeli bureaucracy and personal finance compiled from primary sources. Disclose AI assistance.

Monetisation that pays Israel: Impact.com (ILS, PayPal or wire), PartnerStack (PayPal), Amazon Associates via Payoneer; entry ad network at ~1,000 sessions/month (Journey by Mediavine). Stripe-based programs (Substack, beehiiv, Medium partner) are not usable from Israel.

Loop
1. Keyword gaps with buying intent → 3-5 original guides or calculators per week on the automaton's domain.
2. Weekly: indexed pages, organic visits, affiliate clicks/conversions → revenue_kpi.
3. Double down on converting topics; refresh top pages monthly; prune pages with zero impressions after 90 days.

Expect months. Kill rule: under ₪300 in 30 days after 120 days live.
