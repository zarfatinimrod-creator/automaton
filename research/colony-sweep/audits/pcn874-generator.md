# Refutation audit — the PCN874 generator (`products/pcn874/src/generate.ts`)

Audited at branch `claude/monthly-income-plan-pfs7vu`, head `4ed9d5f`. Method: read the generator, CLI, doc, tests and fixtures; quote the circular (`research/rendered/pcn874-gov-il-874-eng.txt`, cited `ita:<line>`) and the H-ERP manual (`research/rendered/pcn874-h-erp-mirror.txt`, cited `herp:<line>`) for every total the generator computes; build the package (`npm run build`), run `node dist/cli.js generate` on 26 constructed CSVs, and try to get a file out of the package that its own validator would reject. Nothing in `products/`, `docs/`, `skills/`, `logs/` or `.github/` was touched. Baseline: `npx vitest run tests/generate.test.ts tests/cli.test.ts` → 68 passed.

## 1. Verdict

1. **Totals.** All seven computed header totals are **CONFIRMED** on membership, sign and arithmetic against Appendix A and the Table of Values (`ita:107, 109, 114-115, 116-117, 118-119, 120-124, 174, 181-191`). Of the three readings the builder recorded as unsettled: taxable-vs-zero-rated by the VAT being zeros is **CONFIRMED** (`ita:262` vs `ita:283`, `ita:305` vs `ita:326`) and the silent choice is right; credits subtracting from the header totals is **CONFIRMED for the record's sign and UNSUPPORTED as a rule about the totals** — the cited `ita:592-593` says the cancelled invoice is *"reported as an opposite sign"* and nothing about how a header total is reached, so the doc overstates its citation (the silent choice is still defensible, see §2); exports in the zero-value total is **UNSUPPORTED** — no rendered line settles it — and the warning the generator prints matches that grade.
2. **Parsing.** Of 26 constructed CSVs: **3 silently wrong** (a row with more cells than columns — an unquoted thousands separator — writes VAT ₪1 and total ₪800 for a ₪1,800/₪10,000 sale; a row with fewer cells than columns writes a ₪0 zero-rated sale; a quoted comma-decimal `"1800,00"` writes ₪180,000), **2 silent reclassifications** that are documented defaults but move a document between header totals (an empty `vatSum` on a sale; a VAT that rounds to zero), **1 warned but under-described** (Hebrew in `refGroup` writes a 62-byte record the validator counts as 60), **12 refused with a clear message**, **0 crashes**, the rest correct.
3. **A path to a rejected file.** None through the CLI: `writeFileSync` runs once, only when `ok && text !== null` (`src/cli.ts:260-262`), and `--json` on a refusal carries no record text (verified: the refused record string does not appear in the JSON). Two qualifications the builder's claim does not survive: (a) the **library** result of a post-build refusal carries the refused file byte for byte in `result.validation.parsed.records[*].raw` (rebuilt and printed in §5), so *"there is no other way to get text out"* (`src/cli.ts:259`) and *"that is the only way any caller gets a file out of this package"* (`docs/GENERATOR.md:7`) are false for `generatePcn874`; (b) a refusal leaves a **stale earlier output** at `--out` untouched while printing *"nothing was written to"* it. And a scope point that bounds the whole safety story: the validator *"cross-checks no amount at all"* (`src/validate.ts:766`, `docs/GENERATOR.md:216-217`), so every silently-wrong file in §4 **passes self-validation** — the refusal guarantees layout and record counts, not the amounts.

### The three readings the builder recorded as unsettled

**Reading 1 — taxable vs zero-rated is decided by the VAT being zeros (`docs/GENERATOR.md:107-111`, citing `ita:271-283`).** Appendix C's regular `S` row (`ita:250-270`) reads `Customer` / `V` / `Zeros/V` / `V` / **`V`** / `V` / `V` / `Zeros` / `A`; the row headed `Zero Value/Exempt "SL" –` / `not export` (`ita:271-291`) reads `Customer` / `V` / `Zeros/V` / `V` / **`Zeros`** / `V` / `V` / `Zeros` / `A`. The fifth cell is `Sum of` / `VAT` (`ita:241-242`). The same contrast holds for `L` (`ita:305` `V` vs `ita:326` `Zeros`). Nothing else in the two rows differs. **CONFIRMED.** The generator's silent handling matches the grade. `docs/SPEC.md:584-586` records the same reading, citing `ita:283, 326`.

**Reading 2 — a credit subtracts from the totals (`docs/GENERATOR.md:112-115`, `src/generate.ts:696-697`, citing `ita:590-594`).** Verbatim: `•   Detail Amendment "procedure" for an invoice included in a previously submitted` / `report or in a current report.` / `A.   The incorrect invoice will be cancelled – reported as an opposite sign. All other` / `details will be identical to those reported.` / `B.   The invoice will be reported again – with the correct details.` That is a rule about the **record's** sign — and so are `ita:145-146` (`Cancellation/credit from supplier or customer –   always` / `in minus`) and the sign table `ita:525-535` (`Sale to the` / `customer` · `Cancellation/credit` / `to the customer` · `Purchase` / `from supplier` · `Cancellation/credit` / `from supplier` · `Zero value` / `field` — `Sign   +   -   +   -   +`). **No rendered line states how a header total is formed from signed records.** The doc's bridge — *"which only leaves the period's totals right if the signed amount is what enters them"* (`docs/GENERATOR.md:113-114`) — is the builder's inference, not the circular's text. The strongest support for the signed sum is structural: the header gives each total its own `+/-` field (`ita:106, 108, 116, 118, 120-122`), which carries no information unless a total can go negative, and it can only go negative if credits subtract. Grade: **CONFIRMED (record sign) / UNSUPPORTED (totals arithmetic)**. Handling: silent. Defensible, because the alternative (sum of magnitudes) leaves the header's sign fields dead; but the doc should say the reading rests on the header's sign fields and on no line, rather than on `ita:590-594` — a doc-only fix, §6.

**Reading 3 — an export (`Y`) is counted as a zero-value sale (`docs/GENERATOR.md:116-120`).** `ita:184` — `Sales – export   Y`; Appendix C's `Y` row (`ita:356-376`) has `Zeros` in the Sum-of-VAT cell (`ita:368`); note D (`ita:563-564`) — `… The sum of the` / `VAT will include zeros (for service without an export entry – the opposite`; the header field (`ita:117`) — `Total of zero value/exempt sales for period   N(11)`; and the zero-rated sale row is headed `not export` (`ita:272`). The heading separates the *document types*; nothing says which types feed the header field. The header has three sale-amount buckets — taxable (`ita:107`), different-rate (`ita:111`, *"Currently zeros"*), zero value/exempt (`ita:117`) — so a `Y` amount that feeds none of them leaves the header's sale amounts short of its sale records; but no line says the buckets must cover the records. **UNSUPPORTED.** The generator's warning `totals.exportInZeroOrExempt` (`src/generate.ts:929-946`) names the reading and the field that would move; that matches the grade exactly. One nit: its `productChoice` says *"line 564"* for the VAT-zeros sentence, which begins on `ita:563`.

## 3. Rounding

**Unit.** The record amounts are whole shekels: `ita:142-144` — `N(9)   Rounded to the nearest shekel – always a positive value`; `ita:148-149` — `Invoice total not incl. VAT   N(10)   Always the 100%, always a positive value, rounded to` / `the nearest shekel`. No decimal position exists in any `N(n)` width of `layout.ts` (header amounts `N(11)`/`N(9)`, detail `N(9)`/`N(10)`). The word "agorot" appears nowhere in the circular (grep for `agorot`, `round`, `nearest`, `whole`, `shekel` returns only `ita:82, 84, 144, 148-149, 587`). The generator reads shekels with an optional decimal part and rounds to a whole shekel on the decimal **string** (`parseAmount`, `src/generate.ts:253-273`; `frac > half` at `:265` compares equal-length digit strings, which is numerically correct). Verified: `1799.50 → 1800` (tie), `9997.49 → 9997`, `565.51 → 566`, `3141.59 → 3142`; `-0.5 → -1`, `-2.5 → -3`.

**The half-shekel tie.** The circular states no direction. The H-ERP manual states rounding but no direction: `herp:1185-1190` — `סכום המע"מ ללא עיגול : בדוח מע"מ מקוון , PCN874 , לפי דרישת רשות ה מיסים התוכנה מעגלת` / `כל סכום בכל תנועת יומן, ומסכמת את הסכומים המעוגלים. כתוצאה מכך, סכומי המע"מ עלולים` / `להיות שונים. ההפרש עלול להיות כמה עשרות שקלים. רשות ה מיסים מודע ים לנושא , וכמובן` / `ההפרש לא צריך למנוע את שידור הקובץ. …` (my gloss: *the VAT amount without rounding: in the online VAT report, PCN874, at the Tax Authority's demand the software rounds every amount in every journal entry and sums the rounded amounts; as a result the VAT amounts may differ, by some tens of shekels; the Authority is aware and of course the difference should not prevent transmitting the file*), and `herp:1404` — `היות ודו"ח מע"מ מדווח עם עיגול לשלים, ירשמו ההפרשים לחשבון ביטול יתרות.` (*since the VAT report is reported rounded to whole [shekels], the differences are posted to a balance write-off account*). The Rivhit manual has no hit for `עיגול`, `מעוגל`, `לעגל`, `אגורות`, `שקלים שלמים` or `עגול`. So: rounding to whole shekels is the circular's; per-entry-then-sum is a vendor's report of an Authority demand; the tie direction is stated by nobody. The builder's "product choice, warned on the row where it decided a digit" (`docs/GENERATOR.md:129-132`; `row.amount.roundingTie`, `src/generate.ts:517-530`) is the honest handling. **CONFIRMED as unsettled; handling matches.**

**Sum of rounded rows vs rounded sum.** The generator sums the rounded per-row amounts (`computeTotals` runs on `ParsedRow.vat/sum`, which are already whole shekels — `:653-654`). That is what `herp:1185-1186` describes. The circular's header lines (`ita:107, 109, 117, 119, 123`) carry no rounding remark at all, so the only statement on the matter is the vendor's. **The file reconciles with itself**: on `rounding.csv` the header reads `taxableSalesAmount 00000009997`, `taxableSalesVat 000001800`, `otherInputsVat 000000566`, and the two detail records carry exactly `000001800/0000009997` and `000000566/0000003142` — sums of the written rows, by construction. `reportedVat` is the one header amount that cannot reconcile, and by design: it is the filer's figure (`00000001234` from the fixture's `1234.49`).

## 5. The refusal check

**By reading.** `text` is assigned once: `text: ok ? text : null` (`src/generate.ts:1008`), with `ok = validation.counts.error === 0` (`:1004`). The two early returns go through `refuse()` (`:1018-1029`), which sets `text: null` and `validation: null`. The CLI writes in one place (`src/cli.ts:260-266`), guarded by `result.ok && result.text !== null`. There is no temp file, no rename, no stream. Confirmed.

**By trying to defeat it.**

- *A file written before validation:* none. `validatePcn874` runs at `:1003`, the CLI's write at `cli.ts:262` reads `result.text`, and nothing writes between them.
- *A partial file after refusal:* none from a refusal. From an **I/O failure** (`cli.ts:262-266`), `writeFileSync` can leave a partial file when the write itself fails mid-way (disk full); the exit code is 2 and the message says *cannot write*, so this is visible, not silent. A write-to-temp-then-rename would close it; low priority.
- *A stale file after refusal:* **yes** — demonstrated above. Not written by this run, but present, and it is the file a script that ignores exit codes will upload. The REFUSED line is literally true and practically misleading.
- *`--json` carrying refused text:* **no** for the CLI. On the post-build refusal of case f, `--json` prints `written: false`, `out: null`, `problems`, `findings`; the refused detail record `S000000000…` occurs zero times in the output (grep count 0). `Finding` (`src/validate.ts:51-70`) carries rule, record label, line, field, message, sources, `officialText`, `openQuestion` — no raw text.
- *The library API:* **yes**. `GenerateResult.validation` is the full `ValidationResult`, whose `parsed.records[*].raw` is every record of the refused file (`src/parse.ts:30`). Run against case f: `r.ok === false`, `r.text === null`, and `r.validation.parsed.records.map(x => x.raw).join('\n')` reproduces the three refused records exactly (`O514457282…+00000001800` / `S000000000202601120000000000000000001800+0000010000000000000` / `X514457282`). So *"text is the only way any caller obtains a file"* (`tests/generate.test.ts:295-296`), *"There is no other way"* (`README.md:147`), *"that is the only way any caller gets a file out of this package"* (`docs/GENERATOR.md:7`) and *"there is no other way to get text out"* (`src/cli.ts:259`) are all false for the library. The property that is actually structural is narrower: **the CLI writes nothing the validator rejects.**

**What the guarantee covers.** The validator errors on layout, types, counts and Appendix C's zero/counterparty rules; it *"cross-checks no amount at all"* (`src/validate.ts:766`; `docs/SPEC.md:348-354` gives the reason — the H-ERP rounding tolerance). Therefore a file whose amounts are wrong by a column shift (c2, l) or a locale (n) is **not** a file "the validator rejects", and the refusal claim is silent about the case that matters most to a filer. This is not a defect in the validator (its silence on amounts is justified in SPEC §5.2); it is a boundary the generator's doc should state next to the claim.

## 6. Findings

### Requiring a code change, most severe first

1. **Ragged rows are accepted silently** (cases c2, l). `parseRows`, `src/generate.ts:463-466`: cells are read by index and a row with more or fewer cells than the header is neither refused nor warned; extra cells vanish, missing cells become `''` which `parseAmount` turns into `0` (`:255`). Consequence: a plausible file with exit 0 and wrong amounts in fields the circular defines as `Rounded to the nearest shekel` (`ita:142-144, 148-149`). **Fix:** in `parseRows`, before anything else, `if (row.cells.length !== columns.length)` add an error `csv.row.cellCount` naming the line, the counts and the likely cause (an unquoted comma in an amount); if Excel's trailing empty cell is to be tolerated, allow exactly one extra cell that is `''`. Add c2 and l as fixtures that must be refused.
2. **Every comma in an amount is stripped** (case n). `parseAmount`, `src/generate.ts:254`: `raw.replace(/[\s,₪]/g, '')` makes `"1800,00"` read as ₪180,000 and `1.000` as ₪1. **Fix:** accept a comma only as a thousands separator — e.g. `^[+-]?(\d{1,3}(,\d{3})+|\d+)(\.\d+)?$` after stripping whitespace and `₪` — and refuse the rest with `row.amount.unreadable`, whose message should name the comma-decimal case. A number matching `,\d{2}$` with no `.` is the signature to catch.
3. **The refused file is recoverable through the library** (§5). `generatePcn874` returns `validation` intact on a post-build refusal (`src/generate.ts:1010`). **Fix (code):** on refusal return the validator's `findings` and `counts` but not `parsed` — e.g. `validation: ok ? validation : { ...validation, parsed: { ...validation.parsed, records: [] } }` — or change the type so a refused result carries `findings` only. **Fix (doc, if the code is kept):** correct the four sentences listed in §5 to say the CLI writes nothing the validator rejects, and that a library caller can reconstruct a refused file from `validation.parsed`.
4. **A stale `--out` survives a refusal unmentioned.** `runGenerate`, `src/cli.ts:258-267`: nothing looks at the path on refusal. **Fix:** on `!result.ok`, if `existsSync(out)`, append to the REFUSED line *"an earlier file is still at <out>; this run did not produce it"* — do not delete a file the user may own. Cheap, and it removes the one practical way a refused run still uploads something.
5. **Non-ASCII in `refGroup` changes the record's byte width without a warning saying so** (case d). The generator pads by code units (`:660`) and the validator measures the same way (`src/validate.ts:324`); a two-letter Hebrew group makes a 62-byte record under UTF-8. The circular says the file is *"of a fixed structure"* (`ita:40`) and states no encoding (`docs/SPEC.md:390-404`). **Fix:** after building `text` (`:1001`), if `Buffer.byteLength(text, 'utf8') !== text.length`, add a generator **warning** `file.byteWidth` naming the records and byte counts, citing `ita:39-42` and SPEC §5.7 — a warning, not an error, because the encoding is genuinely unstated. `docs/GENERATOR.md:62` should carry the same sentence.
6. **An empty `vatSum` on a sale silently reclassifies it** (case o). **Fix:** in `parseRows`, when `type.side === 'sale'` and the raw `vatSum` cell is `''` (not an explicit `0`), add a warning `row.vatSum.empty` saying the document was counted in `Total of zero value/exempt sales for period` (`ita:117`). Explicit zeros stay silent, so a ledger that writes `0` is not spammed.
7. **A VAT that rounds to zero flips the sale's bucket** (case q). **Fix:** when the raw VAT is non-zero and the rounded VAT is `0`, add a warning `row.vat.roundedToZero` with the same `productChoice` shape as the tie warning (`:517-530`), because here too rounding decided a header total.
8. **An all-empty row is refused as `entry type ""`** (case k). `parseCsv`, `:328`: only a one-cell blank row is dropped. **Fix:** drop a row whose every cell is `''` (or report `csv.row.empty` as info); Excel writes such rows routinely.
9. **A directive without a colon becomes a comment, then "not supplied"** (case h3). **Fix:** in the preamble loop (`:757-777`), when the first word of a `#` line normalises to a `GENERATOR_DIRECTIVES` name but the colon regex fails, add an error `meta.malformed` naming the line and the expected `# name: value` form.
10. **`--reported-vat ""` overrides the CSV and then reads as absent.** `src/cli.ts:253-256`, `src/generate.ts:879`. **Fix:** treat an empty CLI value as absent (`reportedVat === '' ? {} : { reportedVat }`), or say *"the --reported-vat value is empty"*. Trivial.

## 7. What the rendered texts do not settle

Stated as open; none is resolved here by picking a reading.

1. **How `reportedVat` is reached.** `ita:125-126` define the field and sign; no line in Appendix A, B or C, and neither manual, gives the arithmetic (`docs/SPEC.md:341-354`). The closest thing is the H-ERP screen description at `herp:1114, 1116` (`docs/SPEC.md:415`), a vendor's UI, not a rule.
2. **Whether `Y` feeds `Total of zero value/exempt sales for period`** (`ita:117`). The `Y` row's VAT is `Zeros` (`ita:368`, `ita:563-564`); the zero-rated sale row is headed `not export` (`ita:272`); nothing maps entry types to the header's amount fields.
3. **Whether a header total is the signed sum of its records.** Signs on records are stated (`ita:145-146`, `ita:535`, `ita:592-593`); the header totals have sign fields (`ita:106, 108, 116, 118, 120-122`); the arithmetic between the two is not written.
4. **Which way half a shekel rounds.** `Rounded to the nearest shekel` (`ita:144, 148-149`) and nothing more; `herp:1185-1190, 1404` add only that rounding is to whole shekels.
5. **Whether the header is the sum of rounded records.** Only the vendor says the Authority demands it (`herp:1185-1186`); the circular's header lines carry no rounding remark.
6. **The alphabet and encoding of an `A(n)` field**, and therefore whether a non-ASCII `refGroup` breaks the *"fixed structure"* (`ita:40`) in bytes — `docs/SPEC.md:390-404`.
7. **Whether a taxable sale whose VAT rounds to zero is a "zero value" sale for `ita:117`.** Appendix C's test is on the file's VAT cell (`ita:283`), which is zeros either way; the circular never says whether classification precedes or follows rounding.
8. **The record separator and trailing newline** (`docs/SPEC.md:355-361`); the generator writes LF without a trailing newline and says so (`docs/GENERATOR.md:140-145`).
