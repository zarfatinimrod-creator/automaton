## 2. Is the pipeline actually software-operable? — YES, and this is the one CODE-grade fact here

Contributor ingestion on the major agencies is **FTP/SFTP with a metadata CSV**, not a human web upload.
That is exactly the shape a headless agent can drive. GitHub `search_code` for the Adobe host name returned
15 independent repositories hard-coding it:

- `sftp.contributor.adobestock.com` : 22 (SFTP) — in `brejoc/stockuploader` (`main.go`, `stockuploader.toml.example`),
  `Majboor/adobe/upload.py`, `metapropage/metapro/pages/sftp.py`, `DannyJPN/PhotoBankingScripts`
  (`uploadtophotobanks/uploadtophotobanksslib/constants.py`, which also records supported formats
  `.jpg .ai .eps .mp4 .mov`), `eitan567/OSTools/backend/adobe-uploader.py`.
- `samjan190799-cmyk/StockFlow.ios/AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` tabulates the whole set:
  Shutterstock `ftp.shutterstock.com` FTPES:21 · Adobe `sftp.contributor.adobestock.com` SFTP:22 ·
  Alamy `ftp.upload.alamy.com` :21 · Dreamstime `ftp.upload.dreamstime.com` :21 · 123RF `ftp.123rf.com` :21.
- `samjan190799-cmyk/StockFlow.ios/Sources/Models.swift` adds Getty/iStock `ftp.gettyimages.com` and
  Freepik `sftp.contributor-ftp.freepik.com`.
- `metagenp-ctrl/lang-engine/file-handler.js` adds Vecteezy `ftp.vecteezy.com` and VectorStock `ftp.vectorstock.com`.
- `brejoc/stockuploader` README (RENDERED): a CLI that uploads images and video to Adobe Stock over SFTP,
  credentials from flags or a TOML file. Grade: CODE/RENDERED.

**Note on hygiene, not on the finding:** several of those repos have live SFTP usernames and passwords
committed in plaintext (`eitan567/OSTools/backend/.env` holds nine Adobe contributor accounts). I did not
copy them and they are not evidence of anything except that this niche is operated carelessly. If anything,
it is a warning about the calibre of the competition.

**What SFTP does not settle:** whether the AI-labelling flag can be set over SFTP+CSV at all, or only in the
web dashboard. Adobe's requirement as reported is a *checkbox in the Metadata panel*; Freepik's is a *keyword*,
which a CSV can carry. If Adobe's flag is dashboard-only, an honest pipeline needs a human or a browser agent
per batch, and the "zero manual ops" claim breaks. **This is the decisive open question and I could not
resolve it** — the page is blocked. Anyone continuing here should open the Adobe FTP/CSV documentation first.

## 3. Earnings evidence — what is actually documented, and what is not

- **Adobe Stock royalty rate: 33% of net.** Reported floors: $0.33 per download for a new contributor,
  $0.38 once past 10,000 lifetime sales. A typical subscription download works out near **$0.99**.
  Grade: SNIPPET (helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/royalty-details.html and
  contributor.stock.adobe.com/royalties, neither fetchable here).
- **Freepik: $0.04 to $0.07 per download**, PPD model on net subscriber revenue, "multiple independent
  sources, 2024-2026". Grade: SNIPPET, and the sources named are blogs (autokeyworder.com,
  geniusfirms.com), not Freepik. **This is two orders of magnitude below Adobe per download.**
- **Payout minimum: US $25**, and the first sale must be at least 45 days old before any payout can be
  requested. Grade: SNIPPET (helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/getting-paid.html).
- **What I could NOT find:** a single credible, dated figure for what an AI-only portfolio actually earns per
  month. The search surfaced one Medium post ("How I Earn Money on Adobe Stock Contributor with AI-Generated
  Images", Zeeshan Tejani, Oct 2025) and the search tool itself reported that it "doesn't provide specific
  monthly earnings figures". A self-published Medium testimonial is not evidence, and I am not going to
  launder one into a number. **Every ILS ceiling in my findings is an inference, not a measurement, and is
  marked low confidence.**

Arithmetic the colony can check, stated as arithmetic and not as a finding: at $0.33-0.99 per Adobe download
and ~3.7 ILS/USD, hitting **20,000 ILS/month from Adobe alone requires roughly 5,500-16,000 downloads every
month, forever**. That is a top-tier contributor portfolio, built over years, in a category the agency now
floods with AI submissions. Freepik at $0.04-0.07 needs 75,000-135,000 downloads a month. This criterion
does not reach the mission target. It could plausibly reach hundreds of shekels.

## 4. Payability to Israel

**YES for Adobe Stock**, with the caveat below.

- Adobe pays contributors through **PayPal, Payoneer or Skrill** only; minimum US $25.
  Grade: SNIPPET (helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/getting-paid.html). The snippet explicitly says
  "Payoneer is available for contributors outside of the United States" and that Adobe's own docs do not
  name Israel either way.
- **Payoneer serves Israel** — receives in ILS, withdraws to a local Israeli bank, typically within 24h,
  $50 minimum per withdrawal. Grade: SNIPPET (payoneer.com/receiving-accounts/ plus
  community.payoneer.com discussion 67926). Payoneer is itself an Israeli-founded company with Israeli
  operations, which makes a NO implausible.
- **PayPal in Israel** withdraws in ILS via an Israeli-issued Visa card, ~$750/day limit.
  Grade: SNIPPET (leverage.it, illuminea.com — both third-party Israeli consultancies, not PayPal).

So the rail exists and it is the same rail our other lines would use. **The payability gate is not what
kills this criterion; the ceiling is.**

Freepik / Dreamstime / Vecteezy payout methods for Israel: **UNKNOWN**. I spent the budget on Adobe because
Adobe is the only one with a per-download rate worth chasing.

## 5. Owner blockers — the one-time human steps, precisely

These are legitimate KYC/identity steps and nothing more. None of them is ongoing work.

1. **Create the Adobe Stock contributor account** under the owner's Adobe ID and accept the contributor terms.
   A person accepts a contract; an agent must not.
2. **Sign form W-8BEN** (foreign individual) or W-8BEN-E (foreign entity). Until it is validated Adobe
   withholds at the maximum US rate; the Israel-US treaty rate applies only once it is on file.
   Grade: SNIPPET, helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/payment-taxes/tax-faq-non-us-citizens.html.
3. **Open and KYC-verify a Payoneer (or PayPal) account** and link it in the Adobe contributor dashboard.
   The snippet notes these KYC checks are separate from Adobe's and can hold funds.
4. **Possibly: tick the generative-AI checkbox per batch in the Adobe dashboard**, if that flag turns out not
   to be settable over SFTP+CSV. Listed as *possible* because it is unresolved (§2). If it is real it is a
   recurring manual step and it disqualifies the line under MISSION, and it must be checked before any build,
   not after.

I am not listing "model releases" as an owner blocker: the correct response is to generate no identifiable
people at all, which is a build constraint, not a human task.

## 6. The constitutional hazard in this niche — read this before anyone proposes a build

The standard tooling in this market **instructs contributors to conceal AI origin from the platforms that
ban it.** From `KavinthaD/StockGenius-AI-Stock-Guide` (RENDERED, raw.githubusercontent.com,
`METADATA_STANDARDS.md` and `llms.txt`), a public repo backing a commercial service at stock-genius.site:

> **Shutterstock**: "Strictly **NO AI mentions** in any field" / "Assume real photography; strictly NO AI
> mentions in any field."
> **Vecteezy**: "**No AI:** Strictly forbid any AI-related terms in metadata."

Shutterstock bans AI submissions outright. "Assume real photography, no AI mentions" is therefore an
instruction to launder banned content past moderation. A search snippet also reports Shutterstock now scans
for C2PA credentials, IPTC `DigitalSourceType`, XMP from known generators and EXIF software tags before
human review, with three strikes in 12 months ⇒ permanent ban (grade: SNIPPET, and from
aimetadatacleaner.com — a vendor selling metadata *stripping*, so read as a hostile witness describing the
defence it sells against).

**This is RED under our constitution and it is not a close call.** Uploading AI work to Shutterstock, Pond5,
Vecteezy or anywhere else that forbids it — labelled or stripped — is deceiving the buyer and the platform.
The colony must never build it, and must never install a tool whose purpose is stripping AI provenance.
The Vecteezy line is subtler: Vecteezy *accepts* AI with a checkbox, and the repo above still says to keep AI
terms out of the keywords. If that is Vecteezy's own rule it is fine; if it is advice to game search, it is not.
Unresolved, so Vecteezy is AMBER and not buildable until read from Vecteezy's own page.

## 7. The price floor, for context

`neutraltone/awesome-stock-resources` (RENDERED, raw.githubusercontent.com): ~100+ free resources listed
against **6 paid** (Adobe Stock, Fotolia, Shutterstock, Stocksy, GraphicRiver, Inky Deals) — roughly 94% free,
dominated by CC0 (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay) and CC-BY. That is the finding rather than the directory:
**the substitute for a generic stock image is free and always has been.** Generic AI-generated stock competes
against zero. Only specificity that free libraries lack — and that is not what a prompt-to-SFTP pipeline
produces — has pricing power. Cite this as a DIRECTORY; it proves the resources exist, not that anyone buys.
