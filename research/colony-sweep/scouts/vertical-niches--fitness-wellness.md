### Israeli regulatory hook — the one genuinely differentiated thing I found
- [SNIP] `תקנות מכוני כושר (רישוי ופיקוח) (הצהרת בריאות ותעודה רפואית), תשע"ו-2015` — https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/501_287.htm ; parent law `חוק מכוני כושר (רישוי ופיקוח), תשנ"ד-1994` — https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/p212m1_023.htm
- [SNIP] Substance quoted in the snippet: a gym may admit a trainee only after a signed medical questionnaire / health declaration; the declaration must be re-supplied **once every two years**, and where a medical certificate applies, **once a year**.
- Why it matters: this is a *recurring, legally compelled* form event per member — exactly the shape a software-only subscription fits, and it is Hebrew-and-Israel-specific, so US incumbents do not serve it.
- **URL a human must open to close this claim at FETCH grade:** https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/501_287.htm (blocked here).
- Competition on that exact form already exists and is visible in the same search: https://www.2sign.co.il/טופס-הצהרת-בריאות/ (חתימה ירוקה, e-signature vendor), https://tofes101.co.il/forms/gym-health-declaration/ , https://fillfaster.com/template/הצהרת-בריאות-חדר-כושר-משרד-הבריאות/ , https://easydo.co.il/ , plus a free PDF at https://www.sport-tc.com/upload/1513247343.pdf . Free PDF templates are the real price anchor: the buyer's alternative is "print the ministry PDF", which is cheap and legal.

## Judgements

1. **Hebrew health-declaration & waiver SaaS for Israeli gyms/studios** — the only finding with a defensible, non-generic hook (a legally recurring form). But the buyer's alternative is a free ministry PDF and several Israeli e-signature vendors already sell it. GREEN, payable, ~30h, but the honest ceiling is low.
2. **Wix App Market add-on for wellness businesses** — the best *distribution* story (marketplace search does the selling, so the owner never talks to anyone), but blocked on two unknowns: Israeli developer payout, and the $200/month floor which means sub-$200 months pay nothing.
3. **Mindbody ecosystem tooling** — AMBER, do not build. The gate is a human approval plus a paid subscription plus per-studio owner activation.
4. **Shopify booking/membership app** — payability to Israel unresolved and leaning negative. Do not build until closed.
5. **Generic "cheaper than SimplePractice/Jane"** — real pain, but no distribution a silent owner can use, and the field is crowded and funded. Low confidence, low ceiling.

## Dead ends (worth not re-searching)
- Building a full studio-management system for Israel: Arbox plus a 99 ₪ challenger already cover it; not a 40-hour build.
- Hosting open-source EHR/practice-management as a service: manual ops and health-data compliance; incompatible with the mandate.
- Mindbody/Vagaro/Jane API add-ons in general: every one of them gates third-party access behind a manual partner review and a paid subscription on the incumbent.
- Health-data handling for therapists (HIPAA in the US, and Israeli חוק הגנת הפרטיות/medical-secrecy duties): any intake product that stores clinical answers inherits a compliance burden a no-human operation should not carry. Keep any such product to *transport and signature* of a form the business defines, never storage of diagnoses.
