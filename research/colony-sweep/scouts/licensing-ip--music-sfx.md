  does not plagiarise, is not low value, and its AI nature is transparently disclosed in the listing.

### 3. Earnings evidence — thin and anecdotal
Only one figure surfaced: an audio creator reporting USD ~$500 total within 4 months spread across
Unity Asset Store + Unreal Marketplace + GameDev Market + itch.io combined
(https://wowsound.com/game-asset-store-profitable-business , 2021, snippet only — old and single-source).
Snippets also stress that first sales took ~2 months and that social-media marketing drove sales — a
promotion channel the owner cannot operate. No credible public dataset of audio-pack earnings was
found. Anyone claiming a monthly number for this criterion is guessing.

### 4. The AI-native rail
Snippet (weak aggregator, dynamoi): only AI-native platforms (Loudly, SOUNDRAW) accept AI music, and
"streaming distribution through DistroKid ($22.99/yr) or RouteNote remains the most viable revenue
path for AI creators". Uploading volumes of AI tracks to streaming for per-stream royalties is
adjacent to the streaming-fraud pattern platforms are actively purging and is not honest value at
volume; it is not recommended. Spotify/Deezer AI policies were NOT verified here.

## Verdict for the colony

bans exactly the kind of content a software-only operation can make. The tier that permits AI audio
(game-asset marketplaces) permits it only with disclosure, pays 70-88%, has no verified Israeli payout
evidence, and has no earnings evidence beyond a single stale anecdote. Nothing here is a confident

test, not an income line.
