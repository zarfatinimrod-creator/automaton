Meet YouTube’s revenue thresholds for payment
You're in the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) and have continued making cool content and building your audience. Now, you might be wondering, "When do I actually get paid?" There are a few revenue milestones called "thresholds" that you need to meet before you can get paid.

The revenue thresholds you need to meet before getting paid are:

Tax info threshold

Verification threshold

Payment method selection threshold

Payment threshold

Cancelation threshold

Revenue thresholds vary based on the reporting currency listed in your AdSense for YouTube account. When you hit these thresholds, you need to take certain actions to make sure you get paid. You can view the value for all revenue thresholds in the table below.

1. Tax info threshold

If you’re a creator in YPP, then you’ve hit the tax info threshold and need to submit your tax info, no matter your location.

Google is required to collect tax info from creators in YPP. If any tax deductions apply, Google withholds taxes on YouTube earnings from viewers in the US. If you don’t share your tax info with us, Google may be required to deduct up to 24% of your total earnings worldwide.

Review the tax info threshold for your currency and learn how to submit your tax info to Google.

2. Verification threshold

When your YouTube earnings reach the verification threshold, you need to:

Verify your identity and

Verify your address

If you have separate payments accounts for AdSense and AdSense for YouTube, you’ll verify your info when either of your payments accounts reaches the verification threshold. You’ll only have to verify your info once.

Find the verification threshold for your currency.

3. Payment method selection threshold

When your earnings reach the payment method selection threshold, you can choose how you want to get your money. The forms of payment available will depend on your payment address. Learn how to set up your form of payment .

If you have separate payments accounts for AdSense and AdSense for YouTube, you’ll need to select a form of payment for each account when it reaches the threshold.

Find the payment method selection threshold for your currency.

4. Payment threshold

When your earnings reach the payment threshold, then you’ll get paid as long as there are no holds on your account and you’re following the:

AdSense Program policies and

AdSense for YouTube Terms and Conditions or AdSense Terms and Conditions (whichever is applicable)

If you have a single channel associated with your AdSense for YouTube account, you can view your payments in the YouTube Studio mobile app . Find out when you can expect your payment .

If you have separate payments accounts for AdSense and AdSense for YouTube, each account will need to reach the payment threshold to be paid.

Find the payment threshold for your currency.

5. Cancelation threshold

If you decide to close your AdSense for YouTube account and your balance is greater than the cancelation threshold, you'll get a final payment. When you close your account, you’ll get paid within approximately 90 days after the end of the month, as long as you:

Don’t have any holds

Have completed all of the steps to get paid before closing your account
