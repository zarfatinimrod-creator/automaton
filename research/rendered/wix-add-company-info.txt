Add Your Company Info

Your app's listing in the Wix App Market includes basic info about your company.

To review or update your company info:

Go to the Company Info page in your app's dashboard.

Complete the following fields:

Company logo: Square, PNG or JPG format.

Company name: Max 23 characters, including spaces.

Company description: Max 1,200 characters, including spaces.

Company address

Company website

Privacy policy link

Click Save .
