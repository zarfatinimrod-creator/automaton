Privacy Notice

Version 2026-08-17 · Agenthon 2026 — NeurIPS 2026 Competition Track · AH26-POL-03

Policy set: Official Competition Rules ·

Privacy Notice ·
Data & Software Licensing Policy

This Notice explains what personal data Agenthon processes, why it is used, who receives it,
what is made public, how international transfers and retention are handled, and what privacy
rights may apply.

Data-use boundary. Agenthon does not sell participant personal data, does
not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use non-public submissions or
participant personal data to train general-purpose AI models.

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Notice applies to personal data processed for Agenthon 2026, including
registration, team formation, submissions, evaluation, communications, leaderboards, integrity
review, prizes, verification, event participation, and related administration.

Agenthon 2026 is organized by the Society of Quantitative Analysts (SQA) and CEWIT at Stony
Brook University (the “Organizers”), subject to applicable institutional approvals
and any allocation of privacy responsibilities required by law. Primary privacy contact:
[redacted:email]@agenthon.net .

2. Personal data we collect

Category
Examples

Registration and identity

Name, email, country/region when needed, affiliation, public profile URL, account
identifiers.

Team information

Team name, members, captain status, invitations, changes, and track
participation.

Submission and evaluation

Submission files or containers, configuration, provenance declarations, technical
reports, scores, gates, rankings, outputs, and verification materials.

Technical and security

IP address, login/session data, timestamps, device/browser data, execution logs,
audit records, security events, and leakage indicators.

Identity, address or tax residency where needed, payment details handled by the

Biography, public method description, social handle, photographs, or audio/video
where provided or consented to.

Do not place unnecessary personal data, sensitive information, private credentials, or
confidential third-party information in a submission.

3. How we obtain data

Directly from you when you register, join a team, communicate, submit, or provide
verification or prize information.

From your captain or teammates when they manage membership or submit on the team’s
behalf.

Automatically from the Competition Site, platform, sandbox, and evaluation systems as
needed to operate and secure the Competition.

From service providers or public sources when reasonably necessary to verify eligibility,
affiliation, integrity, or prize administration and permitted by law.

Automated technical evaluation generates scores and diagnostics. Decisions with material
consequences beyond the score — such as disqualification, integrity findings, or prize

for credit, employment, lending, insurance, or investment decisions.

5. Who receives personal data

Competition, hosting, storage, authentication, sandbox, and compute providers may process
data needed to operate the Competition.

Organizing Committee members, track leads, evaluators, and integrity/security reviewers
may access data reasonably necessary for their role.

Sponsors and Question Partners do not receive contact details or non-public submissions
merely because they support Agenthon. A designated evaluator may receive limited materials
only when necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Payment processors, accountants, legal advisers, institutional administrators, or
tax/reporting providers may receive information needed for a prize or legal compliance.

Information may be disclosed to authorities or other recipients when reasonably necessary
to comply with law, enforce rules, protect security, or establish, exercise, or defend legal
claims.

6. What we make public

Public leaderboards may display team name, rank, total or per-track score, and relevant
diagnostics.

The permanent record may include final team names, ranks/scores, and, for finalists or
winners, member names, affiliations, prize information, and public method summaries.

A confirmed prize winner may separately publish a reproducibility repository under the
Official Competition Rules .

Non-public source code, containers, model artifacts, verification materials, email
addresses, tax information, and security logs are not made public merely because a
participant enters, is ranked, is verified, or wins.

Event photographs or recordings are used as described when consent or another applicable
basis is obtained.

7. Submission confidentiality and AI training

Non-public submissions and private verification materials are handled as
competition-confidential under the Terms of Participation .
Winning status does not automatically make Organizer-held materials public. A winner’s
open-source package is a separate participant-controlled release.

The Organizers do not sell personal data or non-public submissions, do not use them for
cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not use them to train general-purpose AI models.
Automated systems may process submission content only to score, test, secure, or verify the
Competition.

8. International transfers

Agenthon is operated in the United States and may use providers in the United States or
other countries. Where law restricts international transfers, the Organizers or relevant
provider will rely on an available mechanism such as an adequacy decision, a recognized privacy
framework, Standard Contractual Clauses, a UK Addendum/IDTA, or another lawful mechanism.
Participants may contact [redacted:email]@agenthon.net for
available information about safeguards relevant to their data.

9. Retention

Data
Typical retention approach

Non-winning registration, submission, and routine evaluation data

Generally up to 12 months after the Competition, then deleted or anonymized unless
needed for a dispute, investigation, incident, legal duty, or reproducibility
record.

Finalist/winner verification records

Only as reasonably necessary for final-result auditability, prizes, disputes,
security/integrity review, and announced reproducibility obligations; kept non-public and
reviewed periodically.

Prize and tax records

Generally up to 7 years or another period required by tax, accounting, institutional,
or legal rules.

Public competition record

Team names, final ranks/scores, and published finalist/winner information may be
retained indefinitely as part of the historical and scientific record.

Optional marketing

Until consent is withdrawn, the address becomes undeliverable, or retention is no
longer appropriate.

Security and audit logs

For a period proportionate to security, integrity, incident response, and audit
needs, then deleted or aggregated where practical.

10. Security

The Organizers use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures
appropriate to the Competition, including access controls, sandboxed evaluation, role-based
access where practical, logging, security review, and data minimization. No internet or storage
system is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, portability, or information about processing; to object to certain processing; to
withdraw consent; and to appeal or complain to a privacy regulator. Rights may be subject to
legal exceptions, including preserving Competition integrity, resolving disputes, complying
with law, and maintaining a lawful public scientific record.

To exercise a right, email [redacted:email]@agenthon.net .
Identity verification may be required. Withdrawing optional consent does not affect prior
lawful processing and ordinarily does not invalidate a score or result already published under
another legal basis. Withdrawing an optional consent is as easy as giving it — use the
same email or your account settings.

12. U.S. state notices, age, and external services

Where applicable U.S. state privacy laws provide additional rights, eligible participants
may submit requests through the contact below. Agenthon will not discriminate for exercising an
applicable right, subject to requirements genuinely necessary to provide the Competition or
maintain integrity.

Agenthon is intended for participants who are at least 18 and have reached
the age of majority. The Organizers do not knowingly accept children through the Competition
registration process.

External services — including the competition platform, GitHub, NeurIPS, social
networks, or partner sites — process data under their own notices. NeurIPS registration,
badge, visa-support, conference-access, and venue data submitted directly to NeurIPS or a venue
provider are not automatically part of Agenthon records. Relevant external notices: the
NeurIPS privacy policy and
NeurIPS Code
of Conduct .

13. Changes, contact, and complaints

The Organizers may update this Notice for changes in the Competition, providers, legal
requirements, or data practices. The version and date will be updated, and material changes
affecting registered participants will be communicated through an official channel where
reasonably practicable.
