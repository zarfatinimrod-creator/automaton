Sponsors & organizing institutions

The 2026 challenge is hosted and operated by Yneuro, Inria, and UC San Diego. Track leadership brings together Meta FAIR Brain & AI, Inria, Meta Reality Labs, Muse, Alljoined, Imperial College London, and a wider academic network. Sponsors support the datasets, evaluation, compute, and $20,000 prize program .

Across the challenge, 31 organizers from 15 institutions and 8 countries contribute datasets, scientific leadership, evaluation infrastructure, and senior advice.
