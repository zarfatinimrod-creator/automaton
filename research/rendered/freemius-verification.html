Complete Verification to Start Selling in Production

You can integrate and test your product as soon as you create it to validate your purchase flow without waiting; everything works immediately in the sandbox environment.

However, some products require verification before you can start selling in production.

Submitting the required information usually takes only a few minutes and helps keep the platform safe and reliable for everyone.

Step 1: Go to the Verification Page​

In the Freemius Developer Dashboard, navigate to the product and click Account Verification in the left sidebar to open up the verification form.

Step 2: Fill Out the Verification Form​

Complete each section of the form providing details about your company and product, along with relevant supporting documentation.

Your Files Are Secure

Any files you upload are stored securely using encryption and are not accessible to third parties.

Once you have finished, click the Submit button to have your information reviewed.

Save and Continue Later

You can leave the form anytime and return to complete it later. Your progress will be saved automatically.

Step 3: Wait for Review​

After you submit the form, the page displays your verification status. Check back in a few days to see whether your product has been approved or additional information is required.

If you need help with the verification process, please contact our support team.
