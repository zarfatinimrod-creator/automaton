What if you could create 10 pieces of content in 30 minutes? And actually land leads from TikTok without dancing in your kitchen?


That’s exactly what TJ Robertson is doing — and in this episode, we break down how (I was super curious!).


From walking the dog to generating daily short-form videos, TJ’s content workflow is ruthlessly efficient and powered by AI.


He shares how he uses tools like Descript for super-fast video editing, why TikTok beats every other platform for business leads, and how to nail your hook in the first 3–5 seconds.


We also go deep into the future of search.


Forget “ranking on Google.” TJ predicts traditional search will be obsolete within two years.


Instead, businesses will need to optimise for large language models — think context engineering, structured prompts, and even digital twins that can scale personalised decision-making.


From AI-powered SEO to the spammy ChatGPT tricks you should avoid, this conversation is your crash course in where digital marketing is headed — and how to make sure you don’t get left behind.
