Privacy Notice .

8. Three-stage submission and reproducibility model

Stage
Treatment

1 – Private submission

Ordinary submissions remain non-public and are used only to operate, score, secure,
audit, and scientifically report the Competition.

2 – Private verification

A provisional prize candidate confidentially supplies the source, configuration,
provenance, and other materials reasonably needed to reproduce and audit the
result.

3 – Winner public release

After winning status is confirmed, a team accepting the prize publishes
participant-authored reproducibility code under an OSI-approved license. The original
private submission remains non-public unless separately released.

Participants need not relicense third-party software, data, models, weights, credentials, or
other material they do not own. A permitted non-redistributable dependency must be identified
with a lawful reproduction path. Failure of private verification may affect ranking or prize
eligibility; failure of a confirmed winner to complete the public release may affect prize
payment or acceptance. Neither transfers ownership.
