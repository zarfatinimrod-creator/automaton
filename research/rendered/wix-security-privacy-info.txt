Add Security and Privacy Information

Wix takes the security and privacy of user data seriously. Each time you submit an app for review, we ask you to answer a few questions on how you store, process, and secure user data you collect.

You must complete the Security & Privacy form before submitting a new app, each time changes are made and you resubmit the app, or in cases where the app was published without the completion of this form.

To add your security and privacy information:

Go to the Security & Privacy page in your app's dashboard.

Answer all questions in the form. Question example, "Is access to Wix user data through your networks, operating systems and databases
configured to prevent unauthorised access and changes?"

Check the box at the bottom confirming that your answers are accurate.

Click Save .
