ARC White-Box Estimation Challenge 2026

Given the weights of a randomly initialized ReLU MLP, can you estimate its expected per-neuron activations more accurately than running the network on a comparable number of sampled inputs? The ARC White-Box Estimation Challenge (WhestBench), organized by the Alignment Research Center in partnership with AIcrowd , is a contest in compute-efficient white-box estimation.

Participants build an estimator : executable code that reads a network’s weights and returns per-neuron post-ReLU activation means within a fixed analytical compute budget, metered by flopscope . Submissions are scored on mean squared error against a high-budget Monte Carlo reference, and the contest is set up so that algorithm design — not hardware or implementation tricks — decides the outcome. Any compliant method is allowed: white-box, black-box sampling, or a hybrid. The leaderboard decides.

White-box methods for trained networks are the eventual goal. WhestBench begins with randomly initialized networks, a tractable setting in which to develop the algorithmic toolkit that mechanistic estimation — and the safety questions motivating it — will eventually need.
