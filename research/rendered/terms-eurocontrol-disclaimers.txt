About us

Map designations

The designations employed and the presentation of the material on maps, videos and animations do not imply the expression of any opinion whatsoever on the part of EUROCONTROL concerning the legal status of any country, territory, city or area or of its authorities, or concerning the delimitation of its frontiers or boundaries.

Value Added Tax (VAT) number

EUROCONTROL has no VAT number. As an international organisation, we are exonerated from direct and indirect taxes in our Member States. This is laid down in Article 19 of the EUROCONTROL Convention and the Additional Protocol dated 6 July 1970 to the EUROCONTROL Convention.

The EUROCONTROL identification number in Belgium at the “Banque Carrefour des Entreprises” (ex-Registre National) is: 0923.980.032.

Intra-community transactions

We are not a taxable person in the sense of Article 9 of Directive (EC) 2006/112 (see Article 151.1(b) for more information).

For such transactions, the relevant VAT exemption certificate must be provided to the supplier, together with the contract or the purchase order.

Learn more here

Domestic transactions in Belgium

According to the Belgian VAT Code’s Article 42, paragraph 3.3, the provisions of the Additional Protocol dated 6 July 1970 to the EUROCONTROL Convention and Belgian Ministry of Finance Circular No. 2/1978 dated 3.1.78, the supply of goods and services to EUROCONTROL is VAT exempted for NET amounts (VAT excluded) equal to or greater than EUR 123.95 (see domestic transactions in Belgium).

For transactions in Belgium, the relevant VAT exemption certificate (Form nr 450) must be provided to the supplier, together with the contract or the purchase order concerned.
