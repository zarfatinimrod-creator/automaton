Evaluation
Sim first. Robot final.

RoboSynChallenge evaluation includes one simulation-only preliminary round and one real-robot
final round. Published results report success rate, action steps, and inference time.

Preliminary round Simulation-only evaluation

01 stage

Policies are evaluated in RoboSynChallenge simulation on held-out test settings before finalists are selected.

Simulation only. No physical robot is used in the preliminary round.

Same metrics. Success rate, action steps, and inference time are reported.

Finalists advance. Approximately 30-40 teams move to the real-robot final round.

Final round Real-robot evaluation

02 stage

Finalist policies are run by organizers on the standardized RoboSynChallenge real-robot setup.

Robot only. The final round runs on the real RoboSynChallenge platform.

Same metrics. Success rate, action steps, and inference time are reported.

Final ranking. Published results determine the final leaderboard.
