Safe Wall Magnet Mounting System
Is the mounting system included in the price?
Yes, your order will include both the magnet(s) and a protective leaf for each magnet, depending on the size of your Displate. This is all provided at no extra cost to ensure you can easily display your new Displate on your wall! 😉

What kind of surface do you recommend for mounting your Displates?
Our mounting kit is designed for clean, dry, degreased, smooth, and even surfaces. The effectiveness of the mounting system depends on the surface quality, so avoid textured or uneven surfaces like the ones listed below:

How do I remove the magnet from my wall?
To remove the magnet, simply grasp a corner of the protective leaf and gently pull it away from the wall. It's that easy! 😊

Will removing a mounting system damage my wall?
The protective leaf in our mounting kit has been designed to minimize wall damage when removing the magnet. However, the final outcome may depend on the wall's condition and type.

Can you suggest an alternative method for mounting?
We recommend using our Safe Wall Magnet Mounting System, included with every order at no extra cost, for a secure and damage-free installation. However, if your wall isn't completely smooth and if you're able to drill into the surface, you may also create a hole in the magnet and secure it with a screw for added stability.

Can I mount my Displate on a brick surface?
We don't recommend mounting a Displate on rough or uneven surfaces like brick. However, if drilling is possible, you can create a hole in the magnet and secure it to the wall with a screw.

Can I mount my Displate on a slanted wall?
We do not recommend mounting a Displate on a slanted wall, as the angle may cause the metal plate to detach from the magnet, posing a risk of falling. We advise using a flat, vertical surface for safe and secure installation.

Can I mount my Displate in a bathroom?
While our Displates are galvanized to prevent rusting, we don't recommend placing them in very humid environments like bathrooms, as this could weaken the magnet's strength.

Can I mount my Displate outside?
While Displates are designed for indoor use, they can be placed outside as they are resistant to atmospheric conditions and won't corrode. However, avoid direct sunlight and water exposure, as these can weaken the glue in our Safe Wall Magnet Mounting System. With these precautions, your Displate can be a durable addition to your outdoor space! 😊

Is it possible to mount my Displate on the wallpaper?
We do not recommend mounting Displates on wallpaper. Our mounting kit is designed for clean, dry, degreased, smooth, and even surfaces. The type and quality of the surface greatly affect the effectiveness of our mounting system, so it's important to avoid textured, uneven, or wallpapered surfaces.

Is it possible to purchase additional magnets?
Yes, you can buy additional magnets on our website in packs of 3 or 9. Click here to purchase extra magnets, and here for 3D Magnets.

Is your mounting system reusable?
No, the mounting system cannot be reused once removed from the surface. This applies to both the glue on the leaf and the magnet, as the glue loses effectiveness after removal. If you need an extra mounting kit, please go here . As for 3D Magnet, please go here .

What's a 3D Magnet?
The 3D Magnet is an alternative mounting system that adds depth, giving your Displate a striking 3D effect. It is compatible only with M and L Displates.

What's the difference between matte and gloss finish?
Both finishes will make your artwork stand out, but they each have unique characteristics. The gloss finish is ideal for photographs, as it enhances black shades and boosts colors, creating high-contrast images that are vibrant and striking. On the other hand, the matte finish emphasizes texture and offers a film-like effect. It is resistant to fingerprints, making it suitable for high-traffic areas or places with children. Additionally, it reduces reflections and glare, even in direct sunlight.

What are the image requirements for custom Displates?
To make sure your custom metal wall art looks amazing, we recommend uploading high-quality images that meet the following requirements:

Minimum size: 2880 x 4032 px

Resolution: 300 DPI

File types: JPG, PNG, WEBP, or AVIF

Max file size: 30 MB

Aspect ratio: 1.4:1 (for the best fit)

Avoid adding borders to your image, as these may be trimmed during production. Also, make sure any text or important content is placed at least 200 px away from the edges.

Following these guidelines will ensure your custom metal artwork turns out just as you imagined — sharp, vivid, and ready to impress.

Can I create a Custom Displate if my image is of low quality?
We recommend uploading high-quality images to achieve the best results. Our return policy does not cover custom prints, so starting with a quality image is essential. If your image is not of the highest quality, our upscaler can help enhance it by making the image appear sharper and clearer. However, please be aware that the upscaler cannot perform miracles on extremely low-quality images 😉

How does your upscaler work when uploading a Custom Displate?
Our upscaler enhances your image by increasing its resolution and clarity through advanced algorithms. Unlike simple resizing, this process adds pixels and detail to make the image sharper and clearer. However, if your original image is of very low quality or heavily compressed, the upscaler may not significantly improve it. While it's helpful for minimizing pixelation, it's not a remedy for poor-quality source files. The upscaler technology is provided by Topaz Labs.

What’s the difference between the Blacklight effect and the Glow in the Dark/Phosphorescent effect?
The Blacklight effect reveals enhancements when exposed to UV light, while the Glow in the Dark effect involves a phosphorescent finish that emits light after absorbing it from an external source. For Blacklight, use a UV flashlight with a recommended wavelength of 365 or 385 nm, and avoid prolonged exposure to UV light or direct sunlight to prevent color fading over time.

Glow in the Dark enhancements become visible after being exposed to light for 15-30 minutes and can last up to an hour, depending on the light exposure length and intensity.

Textra
What is Textra?
Textra is a new product line by Displate that enhances your metal art with a 3D print effect. Posters with a Textra finish feature embossed details and tactile 3D-printed contours, combined with a selective gloss and matte finish. This creates a unique, textured effect, resulting in more vibrant and saturated colors compared to regular Displates.

Can I order any Displate with the Textra finish?
Unfortunately, not all Displates are available with the Textra finish. It is currently offered with a curated selection of artwork, including hundreds of best-selling and popular designs. Finding your first textured metal poster should be easy, and we're continually adding more designs to the Textra lineup. You can start your collection with themes like Marvel Superheroes, Inspiring Quotes, or Cute Animals. Explore the full Textra lineup here .

Will there be more Textra posters available?
Yes, we're just getting started! The Textra selection will continue to grow in the coming months. While there's no set schedule for new releases, we plan to add approximately 100 new artworks every two weeks.

How is Textra different from regular matte and gloss Displates?
Both matte (a smooth, standard Displate surface) and gloss (featuring a sleek, reflective finish) posters offer the standard, non-enhanced print on metal. Textra, on the other hand, features some extra enhancements: 3D-detailed contours, tactile textures, and a selective matte & gloss finish – all of which are customized by our Design Studio to match the artworks in the Textra lineup.

Is the Textra finish the same as the limited series Displates enhancements?
No, Textra finish is different from the enhancements in the limited series Displates. Limited Edition and Ultra Limited Edition posters are designed from scratch to push the boundaries of metal art, featuring more pronounced 3D details and premium finishes like screen printing, special inks, varnishes, and gold or holographic foil, etc. In contrast, Textra offers a unified finish with a visibly flatter surface, differing by only a few millimeters. Unlike the limited series, Textra Displates are always available without limited print runs or timed availability.

What sizes are available for Textra?
Textra is available in M-size and L-size Displates.

Can I add gloss finish to Textra?
No. With Textra, the glossy finish is applied only to the selected textured parts of the design. The rest of the poster keeps its standard finish, and a full‑poster gloss layer can’t be added.

Do Textra Displates come in special packaging?
No, Textra posters come in our standard cardboard packaging, just like regular Displates. However, they feature a special black holographic sticker on the back.

Yes, discount codes and special promotions generally apply to Textra Displates unless specified otherwise.

Shards - Limited Drop
What are Displate Shards?
Displate Shards are collectible metal prints - officially licensed artworks printed on real steel in a small, card-sized format, built for collecting and displaying. Every Shard comes with a damage-free magnetic mount, so it is ready to display straight out of the box.

Shards come in three rarity tiers - Common, Rare, and Ultra-Rare - each with its own finish and its own hologram on the back.

There are two ways to get them. In stores, a Secret Drop is a single sealed Shard: you know the collection, and the design is revealed when you open it. On displate.com , a Limited Drop is a full set: you see every design before you buy and choose the rarity tier your set arrives in.

How are Shards different from standard Displates?
Shards are a collector format. Same real steel and same Textra 3D-textured print you know from Displate, in a much smaller size, released as drops rather than as a permanent catalogue, and built in three rarity tiers with different finishes. Standard Displates are made to hang on a wall. Shards are made to collect, display, and build a set.

What size are Shards?
Shards are 8.8 cm x 6.6 cm (3.46 in x 2.60 in).

How does the Limited Drop work?
A Limited Drop is one collection, sold as a complete set on displate.com . You see every design in the set before you buy, and you choose the rarity tier the set arrives in - Common, Rare or Ultra-Rare. What you see is what ships. Each drop is only available while supplies last.

What finishes and rarity tiers do Shards have in Limited Drop?
Three collector-grade tiers, the same in every channel:

- Common - Displate's Textra finish: embossed contours, selective matte and gloss.

- Rare - everything Common has, plus a light-reactive holographic layer.

- Ultra-Rare - everything Rare has, plus hot stamping for maximum shine and a one-of-a-kind metallic look.

Every tier carries a hologram on the back, unique to the rarity. Not every collection has an Ultra-Rare tier - where it does not, that is stated on the collection's page.

How rare is each tier?
Rarity is set per print run: 90% Common, 8% Rare, 2% Ultra-Rare - or 90/10 where a collection has two tiers. In a Limited Drop, you choose the tier your set arrives in, so those figures tell you how scarce the tier is, not what you will receive.

Will there be sets? How do they work?
On displate.com , every Limited Drop is sold as a complete set. A set is one collection built around a single universe, and every Shard in it shares the same rarity tier - the one you choose. Sets differ from drop to drop, and price varies with them, so check the collection's page to see what is in it.

What technical requirements have to be fulfilled by the artwork to pass verification?
- Artwork must be of high print quality

- Accepted formats are JPG, PNG, WEBP, and AVIF, with a size up to 30MB and a minimum 300 DPI

- The shortest side should be at least 2900px with a 1.4:1 ratio for the best fit (ex.,4060px x 2900px)

- Artwork should have no logos, watermarks, or borders

- Text/content should be at least 200px from the edge in a 4060px x 2900px file

- The whole content of the design must be included in one uploaded file

- Multiple uploads of the same image with only slight variations are not allowed

- Tags must directly relate to the artwork's subject.

You can read more information here .

What is a Verified Creator on Displate?
A Verified Creator is a creator who has earned a badge through our verification program by meeting specific criteria. This status highlights your work as high-quality, offering customers a way to discover creative, authentic craftsmanship.

How can I become a Verified Creator?
To become a Verified Creator, you need to meet specific criteria on Displate. This includes maintaining a well-developed portfolio of active artworks, demonstrating consistent creative contribution and engagement with the platform. Keeping your Displate profile and banner picture updated is also important, as it helps present your professional and trustworthy presence as a creator. Your profile name should also represent you as a reliable creator. Using your real name or a recognizable artistic pseudonym helps build trust among customers and make your profile more polished.

You should also publish artworks that comply with Displate’s Terms of Use and upload guidelines, which include, among others, maintaining a diverse collection, avoiding duplicate designs, and repetitive works that are very similar in style, composition, and color palette. Additionally, your artwork must showcase creativity and authentic craftsmanship. Once your account meets these requirements, you could be awarded the Verified Creator badge.

Does being a Verified Creator affect the visibility of my work?
Being a Verified Creator means your work will be included in the ‘By Verified Creators’ filter, allowing customers to search for verified, high-quality art specifically. This can increase the visibility of your work among customers who prefer such an attribute.

Can the Verified Creator status be revoked?
Yes, if any of the required criteria are not met, the system may revoke the Verified Creator status.

Do Verified Creators use AI?
While no reliable technology currently exists to definitively detect AI use in the creative process, the Verified Creators program highlights creators whose work reflects clear human creative direction. Their portfolios showcase meaningful human direction, skill, and craftsmanship, whether created entirely by hand or with assistance from the latest technologies like AI. The Verified Creators list excludes those with portfolios that feature a large number of near-duplicate pieces and repetitive works that share the same style, composition, and color palette, and whose artworks are clearly generated without human creative input. On the contrary, the focus is on creators who integrate human creativity and input in their work.

Can the Community influence who becomes a Verified Creator?
Yes. Our Community Advisory Board (CAB), made up of trusted and dedicated members from our Discord community, plays a key role in shaping the Verified Creators list. They recommend new creators while flagging those who may no longer meet our criteria. This keeps our curated community fresh, diverse, and aligned with what our Collectors value most.
