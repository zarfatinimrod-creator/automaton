Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, straight answers

What services do you specifically offer?

SEO built for AI search, including what the industry calls GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). We also offer video editing and website development. We believe SEO has to account for AI search to remain relevant – every strategy we build helps you rank in both traditional results and AI-generated answers. We create custom packages based on what will actually bring you the most leads per dollar spent.

How do we get started working together?

Right now, the first step is joining our waiting list. We’re at capacity while we hire and train new team members to maintain our quality standards. We reach out to people on the list in the order they joined as soon as we can take on new clients. Once we connect, we’ll have a quick call to see if it’s a good fit, followed by a 90-minute discovery call where we learn everything about your business, create your brand guide, and develop a marketing plan specifically for you.

How much does it cost to work with TJ Digital?

Our monthly retainers start at $1,900. We charge $500 for strategy and account management, with the remainder going toward credits that cover the actual work. See our pricing section for examples of what credits cover.

What makes your approach different?

We focus on efficiency and transparency. We integrate AI intelligently to reduce costs while keeping human oversight. You see everything we’re doing in real-time, and we only recommend strategies that make sense for your specific business.

What if I have more questions?

Just reach out! Call or email us and we’ll answer any questions. No sales pressure – we only want to work with businesses we can genuinely help.

How quickly will I see results?

SEO typically takes 3-6 months to show significant results. However, with AI Search we expect to see results in the first 3 months. We’re honest about timelines and won’t promise overnight miracles. The upside is that the work compounds; content we create today continues working for you months and years down the line.
