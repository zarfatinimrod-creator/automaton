I tested AI-written copy vs human copy on my landing page.

Here's what happened...

I took one of my landing pages and rewrote it three ways:

→ Pure AI (Claude)
→ Pure human (me writing for 2 hours)
→ And hybrid (AI draft + my edits)

Ran each version for 7 days.

Same traffic.
Same offer.
Same everything.

What I tested:

→ Headline variations (3 per version)
→ Body copy flow
→ CTA button text
→ Overall persuasiveness

The results:

✅ Pure AI: Clear, benefit focussed, but felt generic
✅ Pure Human: More personality, but took forever to write
✅ Hybrid: Best of both worlds

Here's what surprised me:

The AI version had the highest initial engagement (people read more), but the hybrid version converted better.

Why?

The AI got attention, but my edits added the emotional hooks that drive action.

Engagement rates:
• AI: 68% scrolled past fold
• Human: 52% scrolled past fold
• Hybrid: 71% scrolled past fold

Time investment:
• AI: 15 minutes total
• Human: 2+ hours of writing + breaks
• Hybrid: 30 minutes (AI draft + edits)

The biggest lesson👇

AI is incredible at structure and clarity.
Humans are still better at emotional nuance.

Combine them and you get copy that's both fast AND persuasive.

My new workflow:

1️⃣ Let AI write the first draft
2️⃣ I add personality and emotional triggers
3️⃣ Use AI again to punch up specific sections
4️⃣ Final human polish
