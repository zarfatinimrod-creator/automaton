Step 2. Set up your payment profile

Quick steps

Add a new payment profile.

You must have a payment profile in order to sell books on Google Play. The country listed in this payment profile will determine the currency you're paid in.

Detailed steps

You'll be asked to set up a payment profile in a pop-up box when you sign up for a new account and agree to the terms. If you skipped this step, go to the Payment Center , and under Payment profiles , click Add a Payment Profile .

Enter your business name, contact name, address, and phone number.

Click the Submit button.

If you clicked Add a Payment Profile in the first step: Edit your sales territory (or create a new one ) and select the new payment profile from the Payment profile drop-down, then click Save Changes .

If you see an error message about expired credentials, please sign out of your Google Account and sign in again.

Go to the Payment Center

You can have multiple payment profiles if you want to receive payments in multiple bank accounts, but you only need one to start selling books on Google Play.
