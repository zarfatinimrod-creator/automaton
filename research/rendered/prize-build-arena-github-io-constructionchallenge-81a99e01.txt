// 04 — Scoring How Scores Are Computed
A valid unpowered payload must complete three revolutions. Then the performance score is multiplied by the Build-mode coefficient.

Performance · 100 pts
Orbital Payload 15%

Deliver more useful payload mass. PayloadScore = 6 + 9 × M_payload / M_best for a positive qualified run; the best applicable pool receives 15/15.

Payload Fuel Efficiency 15%

η = M_payload / F_expended; EfficiencyScore = 6 + 9 × η / η_best. Fuel left on lost hardware counts as expended; fuel brought home does not. A verified legal zero-propellant mission receives 15/15.

Recovery Completeness 30%

RecoveryScore = 30 × validly recovered dry hardware mass / initial non-payload dry hardware mass. Recovery requires ≥90% structural integrity and stable landing without propulsive support for 10 seconds.

Landing Accuracy 40%

Dry-mass-weighted accuracy: land closer to the launch site. Credit falls sharply beyond about 30 game-distance units; lost hardware earns zero.

Mission Rules
Three-orbit gate The payload must fly unpowered through three complete revolutions, with the entire payload strictly above 1100 game-distance units from the planet center during the qualifying interval; otherwise competitive score is 0.

Mission time and cost Mission Time has no competition-imposed maximum or speed component in the main score. Season 02 has no token-cost or API-cost deduction from the main score.

Fuel accounting Fuel expenditure covers the entire mission, including recovery. Fuel remaining in validly recovered hardware is not expended.

PerformanceScore
PayloadScore + EfficiencyScore + RecoveryScore + LandingScore

S02 Validation Rules
01 No manual control in Build with Agent

Agents pilot the submitted machine. Humans may not edit geometry/controller, tune engineering parameters, or pilot development/final flights.

02 Reproducible controller

control/ must explain telemetry inputs, outputs, phases, state transitions, guidance/PID/scripted/hybrid logic, staging, recovery, landing, thresholds, and mission completion; the official S02 entry point must reproduce the run.

03 Evidence integrity

Histories must correspond to machine.bsg and trajectory.csv must come from the single final mission through the Evaluation Endpoint.

04 Rejection conditions

Manual geometry changes, behavior-changing mods, illegal vanilla values, fabricated/manipulated evidence, or a prohibited ready-made machine/controller can be rejected.

05 Official Environment

Use the pinned S02 environment (Besiege Build 24131898) and map. All tracks ban Automation, Surface, and Parachute blocks and parachute-equivalent mechanisms; no illegal parameters, physics changes, or fabricated evidence.

06 Prelaunch Declaration

Declare payload objects, recovery machines, trackers, and mass/fuel inventories before launch. The payload denylist is separate from global block bans; see Kaggle Rules.
