Participate

Getting Started

Participants register a team, receive a unique team ID, download the data and Starter Kit,
generate their model predictions, and submit to the benchmark .

Workflow

Step-by-Step Participation

1

Register Team

Register or manage your team to receive a unique team ID, updates, and access
to submission instructions.

Register or manage
team

2

Receive Unique Team ID

After registration, each team is issued a unique identifier that must be used
for all benchmark submissions and leaderboard entries. Include this team number
and your exact registered team name in the abstract of your workshop paper.

3

Download Data

Download the Spring and RobustSpring training splits needed for
model
development, local validation, and practice runs.

4

Install the Starter Kit

Set up the PTLFlow-based Starter Kit with training and inference scripts,
data loaders, and example configurations.

GitHub Starter Kit

5

Run Baseline

Run the provided baseline configurations to verify your environment, data paths,
and end-to-end inference pipeline.

6

Generate Predictions

Produce model outputs for your chosen task(s) and export them in the
required prediction format.

7

Validate Archive Locally

Package your predictions into a submission archive and run local validation to
catch formatting or naming issues before uploading.

8

Submit to Benchmark Server

Upload your validated archive to the benchmark to receive scores on the
development leaderboard.

9

Submit Workshop Paper and Reproducibility Package

Use the official NeurIPS 2026 paper format with its sglblindworkshop option for
a single-blind submission with
4–6 main-paper pages; references and an optional appendix may follow outside that
limit. Challenge teams must include their exact team number and registered team name
in the abstract. Submit the reproducibility package with the camera-ready paper. See the
Call for Papers for details.
