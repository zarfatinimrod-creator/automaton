Set a custom passcode on YouTube Kids
Set your custom passcode

You can set a custom passcode for access to parental controls and settings in the YouTube Kids app:

Select the Lock .

Complete the multiplication problem.

Select Create custom passcode .

Enter a 4-digit passcode of your choice.

Click SUBMIT .

Change or delete your custom passcode

You can change or delete your passcode in Settings :

Select the Lock and enter your passcode.

Select Change your p asscode to set a new passcode, or select DELETE PASSCODE to remove your custom passcode.

Forgot your passcode?

If you forget your passcode, you can reset it by uninstalling and reinstalling the YouTube Kids app. Keep in mind that you’ll lose existing recommended videos when you uninstall the app.
