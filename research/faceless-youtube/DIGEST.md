## discovery

**Scout summary.** YouTube is not closed to a day-one video the way Gumroad Discover is. I found no documented gate on prior success. YouTube staff say each video is judged by viewer response and that a Home slot is reserved for new channels, but only vendors like vidIQ and TubeBuddy relay this, and I read no primary YouTube page. The one independent measurement (Pew 2018) found 64% of suggestions went to videos with >1M views and 5% to videos with <50k. The honest verdict is that YouTube is open to new videos by an exploration budget of unknown size, so the sibling's "same wall as Gumroad" is a hypothesis, not a finding. What is new: YouTube reports impressions and CTR per video and traffic source to the owner, so this can be measured cheaply. "Low competition" cannot be measured in advance: scraping is banned, the API forbids aggregating other channels and building derived scores (unless audited), and the Trends API is alpha by application. Competition shows up only in our own analytics after publishing. Channel history meets constraint 8 but is weaker than Apify's. Test: 6 long-form videos, read at day 56 and day 112. Kill if stranger impressions stay below 33,000 per 28 days, CTR stays below 2% at 1,000+ impressions, or stranger watch hours fall below 333 at day 112. Qualifying for YPP pays about ₪170–260/month.
