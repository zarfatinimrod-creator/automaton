## 1. What is being judged, and what is not

The 3.9 rejection judged **one-click Shorts/TikTok generators, for a Hebrew audience, at volume**. The reel describes
**one English long-form channel in a high-RPM niche**. The reassessment tests whether that difference changes the
verdict — not whether the 3.9 verdict was wrong (it was not, for what it judged) and not whether the reel is a method
(it is a comment-for-DM funnel from a prompt-pack seller; it "counts for nothing as evidence; only its order of headings
is worth keeping" — prompt-critique #13, UPHELD).

Two framing corrections carried from the audits into this verdict:

- **Portfolio framing.** MISSION requires several lines summing to the target, so the decision row is a **₪5,000 line
  share**, not ₪20,000 (rpm-evidence angle; MISSION.md:35-40).
- **The board is judging a new English proposal, not declaring the 3.9 trigger met** (prompt-critique #14 claim of
  record). Section 2 explains why the trigger as written is the wrong test for this variant.

## 4. What the rendered evidence settled since 3.9 (the facts this verdict rests on)

Payability and money
- Israel is on the YPP list [RENDERED youtube-ypp-availability.txt:175] and on the Creator Partnerships list
  [RENDERED youtube-creator-partnerships.txt:122] — but partnerships require negotiating and signing with the brand
  [:220], which the mandate excludes, and require YPP [:246].
- Thresholds: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months [RENDERED youtube-ypp-overview.txt:89];
  **from 1 Feb 2027, 8,000 hours** for new applicants; existing members unaffected [RENDERED
  youtube-ypp-2027-terms.txt:94]. New activity floor for all members from that date [:102-108].
- Only public long-form hours count; private videos and **ad campaigns do not** [RENDERED youtube-ypp-overview.txt:165,
  :169, :175]. Paid promotion cannot buy the path to YPP.
- Review "typically in about 1 month" [:123], multiple reviews possible [:125]; rejection → appeal within 21 days or
  reapply after 30 days, then 90 [:127]. Inactivity for 6 months can switch monetization off [:141]. Changing privacy or
  deleting videos during review "might impact the status of your application" [:209]. Ads may run on a pre-YPP channel
  with no share to the creator [:219].
- Payment currency is permanent [RENDERED youtube-ypp-payout.txt:178]; threshold rolls over [:102]; withholding can
  start with an investigation [:126]. US tax: form expires after the third full calendar year [RENDERED
  youtube-us-tax.txt:63], check by Dec 10 each year [:61], legal name exactly as on documents [:191], a foreign TIN
  suffices for the treaty claim — no ITIN [:211]; YPP income is "Other Copyright Royalties" [:243] → treaty Art. 14
  10% cap (irs-us-israel-treaty.txt:591-593), creditable in Israel (:855).
- RPM is after YouTube's share and includes unmonetized views [RENDERED youtube-rpm-definition.txt:75, :81]; geography
  and season move it [:171, :169]; the page gives **no** niche or country figures.

Policy
- Persona rule: "channels that use AI-generated personas to deliver information on sensitive topics. This includes any
  content that presents itself as a human expert providing advice … such as health, legal issues, finances, or
  politics" [RENDERED youtube-monetization-policies.txt:244]. "Such as" — not a closed list; "persona" is undefined;
  whether a disclosed unnamed TTS narrator counts is UNKNOWN (REGRADE, prompt-critique C2). The safe design keeps
  the channel off every topic with a plausible advice reading.
- Creator integrity: "be who they say they are and not misrepresent themselves" [:294] → the About section must state
  the channel is AI-produced by an unnamed operator and never imply a human host.
- No workaround channels after demonetization [:288]; reapply path exists [:348].
- Disclosure "won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money" [RENDERED
  youtube-altered-synthetic-disclosure.txt:173]; YouTube may auto-label content its systems detect as AI [:183].

Upload route (the mandate's binding gate)
- Uploads from an unverified API project are locked private [RENDERED youtube-api-videos-insert.txt:219]; a locked
  video cannot be appealed, only re-uploaded via a verified service or the app [RENDERED
  youtube-private-lock-help.txt:84], is invisible [:72] and "not eligible for monetization" [:100]; a lock trigger
  is "uploaded by an unverified third party API service" [:90] — so **T1 must confirm the publisher is audited, not
  only that the upload succeeded**.
- An honest zero-touch uploader on our own project must not "automate or trigger … uploads … without the user's prior
  specific and express consent" [RENDERED youtube-developer-policies.txt:555]; "the user must expressly consent to
  those actions prior to their actual execution" [:381]; "users must have final control over the data that will be
  published" [:209]; YouTube may demand test accounts "within the timeframe stated in that request" [:543] and its
  mail goes to the console login account [:333]. The own-project route therefore needs recurring owner confirmation
  and correspondence → **it violates the mandate and is not proposed**.
- YouTube "may … automatically set some videos to private when we detect that they may have been uploaded and
  published without the channel owner's knowledge or permission" and signs the owner out [RENDERED
  youtube-policy-changelog.txt:342] — a live risk for any zero-touch uploader, and a signal the test must watch.
- Quota is not a constraint: 100 uploads/day at cost 1 [RENDERED youtube-api-videos-insert.txt:235].

Discovery
- Per-video ranking against everything a viewer might watch [RENDERED youtube-impressions-ctr.txt:65]; the
  recommendation signal named is relevance and **average view duration** [:87]; CTR falls as impressions widen [:83];
  half of channels have CTR 2-10% [:81]. Whether YouTube gates on prior success: **UNKNOWN** either way (discovery #3).
  Survivorship-free base rate: median **35 lifetime views** per random public video (McGrady et al. 2023;
  SNIPPET + CODE-secondary). "Low competition" cannot be measured in advance within the Developer Policies
  (discovery #6, #17); demand is measurable only afterwards from our own Analytics.

Production
- Sora is gone [RENDERED openai-sora-discontinuation.txt:11, :13]. Kokoro Apache-2.0 [kokoro-82m-model-card.txt:53,
  :97], hosted rate under $0.06/hour of audio [:95]. Measured: RTF 0.236, 107 ms/frame, encode 0.29× real time; a
  9-minute chart video is 10-25 wall-minutes on 4 cores, ₪0 cash on a free host (production-stack #7, MEASURED;
  host UNKNOWN). MoneyPrinterTurbo is the wrong tool for chart-led long-form (#19).

## 5. Why REOPEN_AS_EXPERIMENT and not the other two

**Not STAY_REJECTED.** Of the five reasons, two do not apply to this variant and three hold only in part (§3). What is
left is not a known failure but a **measurable unknown** — do strangers find one honest English channel, and does a
fully agent-made channel pass human review — and MISSION constraint 7 says that question is answered by the cheapest
test, not by another ceiling. The test costs ₪0 cash, ~60-150 runner-minutes, ~₪50-60 of tokens (8-10 ILS/video,
INFERENCE low) and 20-40 owner minutes once. The expensive risk — the person-scoped monetization consequence [:342]
and AdSense coupling [payout:126] — attaches only at YPP application, which this verdict makes a **separate stage
behind a measured gate**. A false GO under this design costs a dormant brand channel and an afternoon of compute. A
false NO throws away the only variant with day-one measurability (impressions, CTR, traffic source, subscriber split)
that no other platform the colony has tried offers.

**Not REOPEN_AS_LINE.** No niche RPM exists from a primary source; the modal outcome is ₪0 (median 35 views); a
threshold channel earns ₪130-260/month — under the colony's own ₪500 kill floor and 19-29× short of a ₪5,000 share;
first ledger money is ≥8-10 months away in the best case and "11-25 months or never" realistically
(monetization-gates #9); and the stage-B owner steps (AdSense, PIN, tax forms) are not justified by anything yet
measured. A target on this line today would be hope with a number on it.

**What would flip it to STAY_REJECTED:** T1 fails and the owner does not opt into a paid publisher tier or recurring
confirmations; or K0/K3 fire (§10). **What would flip it toward a line:** K3 passes at pace and a YPP review admits the
channel — then, and only then, a ledger row with an AdSense payment id decides.

## 8. Owner steps — exactly, and the minutes after setup

**Stage A (asked now; unlocks the experiment).** Audited estimate 15-25 minutes for A1-A4 (upload-automation #15,
claim of record), plus A2b if no brand Google account exists (~10 min, unaudited estimate). Total **~20-40 minutes once.**

1. **A1.** Google account with 2-Step Verification (may already exist). If that account already has a channel, adding
   another may require advanced features [RENDERED youtube-policy-changelog.txt:332] — tell us if YouTube asks.
2. **A2.** Create the channel as a **Brand Account** under the brand name we supply — never the owner's name; and add
   the colony's manager Google account as a manager (managers use their own accounts, no password sharing —
   [RENDERED youtube-brand-account.txt:61]).
3. **A2b.** If no brand Google account exists: create one (phone verification is why this is his), and hand its
   credentials to the colony as GitHub secrets. It becomes the manager and the mailbox YouTube writes to.
4. **A3.** Phone-verify the channel (SMS) — unlocks uploads over 15 minutes and custom thumbnails (SNIPPET grade).
5. **A4 = T1.** Sign in once to one **audited** third-party publisher on its free tier (Upload-Post is the candidate;
   its audited status and free-tier long-form access are unverified — §9) and connect the Brand channel via its OAuth.
   No paid tier: any subscription is his recurring-cost decision, never the float.

**Stage B (listed so the whole path is visible; NOT asked until K3 passes and the board decides).**
- **B1.** Advanced features via channel history or a one-time ID photo (not a camera video; Israel availability
  UNKNOWN) — reportedly required for monetization (upload-automation #14).
- **B2.** Accept YPP terms in Studio when eligible; choose modules (fan-funding/Commerce Product Module optional).
- **B3.** Create the AdSense for YouTube account **from Studio only** [RENDERED youtube-ypp-overview.txt:83]: legal
  name exactly as on documents, Latin letters [youtube-us-tax.txt:191]; address; enter the **PIN that arrives by post**;
  choose payment method and **currency (permanent)** [youtube-ypp-payout.txt:178] — colony recommendation: USD wire,
  subject to the unrendered Israel rail.
- **B4.** US tax info: W-8BEN, Israeli TIN (a foreign TIN suffices, no ITIN [youtube-us-tax.txt:211]), treaty claim
  Art. 14. Recurs: resubmit every ~3 years [:63]; check by Dec 10 each year [:61].
- **B5.** Tax file — already owner step 2 (`tax-file`, עוסק פטור); at target income עוסק מורשה with periodic VAT
  (tiktok/01:375-378). Shared with every line, not specific to this one.

**Not owner steps, by decision:** own Google Cloud project + API audit (needs per-item/batch confirmation and audit
