**New facts nobody recorded:**
- From 1 Feb 2027 all YPP channels, including existing ones, face new 'active' rules. A channel is active only with '1,000 qualified watch hours in the past 365 days', '1 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days', or '2 long-form videos or 5 Shorts uploaded every 90 days'. Channels that drop below get a 90-day restore window (research/rendered/youtube-ypp-2027-terms.txt:102-116). This adds a maintenance floor that grandfathering does not remove.
- From 1 Feb 2027, earning from the Shorts Creator Pool requires 10M qualified Shorts views over the last 90 days, even for existing YPP members. Channels below it stay in YPP and keep long-form earnings (youtube-ypp-2027-terms.txt:82; youtube-blog-ypp-2027.txt:120). A small Shorts-heavy channel earns nothing from Shorts.
- Private, unlisted and deleted videos and ad campaigns do not count toward YPP watch hours; only 'Qualified watch hours gained from long-form videos you’ve set public' do (youtube-ypp-overview.txt:165-179). Uploads forced private by an unaudited API project therefore accrue zero qualifying hours, which confirms the API audit is on the YPP critical path.
- YPP rejection is not final. 'you can appeal the decision within 21 days or keep uploading original content and you’ll be able to re-apply after a 30-day period'. After a second rejection the wait is 90 days (youtube-ypp-overview.txt:127). This quantifies the reapplication cadence.
- Monetization can be switched off for inactivity: 'we may turn off monetization on channels that haven’t uploaded a video or posted to the Posts tab for 6 months or more' (youtube-ypp-overview.txt:141, repeated :213).
- Changing video privacy or deleting videos after applying 'might impact the status of your application' (youtube-ypp-overview.txt:209). A pipeline that bulk-privates or deletes weak videos during review risks the application.
- Premium revenue split: creators share 30% of net Premium and 60% of net Premium Lite subscription revenue, then 55% for long-form and 45% for Shorts. Premium Lite is expanding to all Premium countries (youtube-blog-ypp-2027.txt:114-115; youtube-ypp-2027-terms.txt:72-74). This is a non-ad component inside RPM.
- Per YouTube's own definition, RPM is total revenue after YouTube's revenue share, per 1,000 views, and 'includes all views, including ones that weren’t monetized'. It includes Premium, memberships, Super Chat and Super Stickers (youtube-rpm-definition.txt:65, :75, :89-93). The ledger's arithmetic (views = income / RPM x 1000) therefore uses the right metric, and no ad-fill discount needs to be applied on top.
- The first-party RPM page gives no niche or country RPM figures. Its only dollar figures ($7 total, $3.50 CPM, $4.67 playback-based CPM) are a worked CPM arithmetic example (youtube-rpm-definition.txt:157) and must not be read as benchmarks. The scout's blocker ('no RPM definition could be read first-hand') is now resolved, but no niche RPM is established.
- Tax-form review 'may take up to 7 business days'. A declined form (TIN or name not matching) is re-notified and must be resubmitted (youtube-us-tax.txt:271-283). Tax-form fields accept only Latin letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and ampersands (youtube-us-tax.txt:81, :139).
- Earnings can be withheld or deducted from the start of a policy investigation: 'Withholding and/or deductions may begin from when Google initiates its investigation of potential violations' (youtube-ypp-payout.txt:126; adsense-payment-7164703.txt:107). Revenue from a channel later found in violation is not safe money.
- Before YPP, YouTube may serve ads on the channel and pay the creator nothing: 'If you do not currently meet the higher eligibility criteria of YPP you may still see ads being served on your content. In this case, you are not yet eligible to receive a share of the revenue.' (youtube-ypp-overview.txt:219).
- Treaty Art. 14(3): the 10% royalty cap does not apply if the royalty is attributable to a US permanent establishment of the recipient, in which case Art. 8 business profits applies (irs-us-israel-treaty.txt:605-608). This is not relevant absent US activities, but it is the condition under which the 10% scenario fails.
