# Terms audit of every site active in `research/rendered/urls.txt` (29.9.2026, tick 20)

**Why.** The render-watch runner fetches every active line weekly. On 28.9 TikTok's terms and on 29.9 Gumroad's terms were found to bar automated access only after the runner had fetched their pages (`logs/CHANNEL_LOOP.md` §9). This audit checks the other 81 sites against their own terms.

**How.** Four Opus auditors (about 20 sites each), each checked by an adversarial Opus verifier (`wf_70374649-7be`). Each verdict rests on terms text in the repo (rendered), or on a GitHub-hosted copy (github grade), quoted with a citation. The main thread re-read the PayPal, Indiebook and Teach Simple clauses in the captures before acting.

**Verdicts.** BARRED: the terms forbid automated access with no exception that covers the runner. CONDITIONAL: allowed on a condition (robots.txt, reasonableness, a purpose). NOT_BARRED: terms read, no bar. NO_TERMS_CAPTURE: no terms text for the site exists in the repo.

**Actions taken (29.9).** Every BARRED site, and every CONDITIONAL site whose condition the runner does not meet, is in `TERMS_BARRED` in `scripts/render-watch.mjs`, refused at parse time and as a redirect. Its lines in `urls.txt` are commented out `# paused (terms audit, 29.9.2026)`. That covers 66 lines. Google's help and developer pages are included because the runner does not read robots.txt, which Google's terms make the condition. Mozilla's add-on API is paused because its acceptable-use policy bars harvesting account names and email addresses; the four stored AMO results were redacted (authors, support emails, and emails in text), though the earlier versions stay in git history. Topcoder's one line is retired, since Topcoder was killed in tick 18. For each NO_TERMS_CAPTURE site with a known terms URL, the terms page is queued. The rule is that a site's terms page may be fetched once so its terms can be read. **Before the next weekly run (Tuesday 6.10), every NO_TERMS_CAPTURE site must have its terms read or its lines paused.** Whether the runner may fetch a site whose terms are unread or silent is put to FABLE_QUEUE row 16(d).
