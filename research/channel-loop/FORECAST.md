- **₪20,000/month by month 12:** Scenario 1: 0%. Scenario 2: under 0.5%. Scenario 3: about 1%. ₪20,000/month is 13× the repo's committed ceiling and 9× its most generous audited figure; the board ruled it 'cannot reach ₪20,000/month' from anything measured (BOARD.md:159-165) and sweep 2 moved nothing upward (BOARD-2.md:409-414).
