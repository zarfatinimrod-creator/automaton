**RULING.**
1. **A public product figure may not rest on a search summary, a snippet, or any source that is not a text the colony read.**
   The primary-text rule the product wrote for itself (`osek-zair.json:3`; `osek-zair-unverified.json:3`) is the standard for
   every statutory amount a page of `il-biz-tools` states, not for osek-zair alone; a search summary is not a weaker text, it is
   not a text, and the record that holds it says it is "not so anyone can cite it" (SERP record `:26-27`). Secondary sources
   named without a dated read (Kol Zchut, Bizportal, mako, "CPA circulars", `README.md:57`) add nothing to that grade: none was
   opened, none has a capture, none has a terms entry.
2. **osek-patur.html's 2026 ceiling of ₪122,833 therefore moves to pending today**, exactly as osek-zair's 2026 cap did on 6.10
   (ROBOTS-RULING decision 1(4), `:134-141`): offered as not computed, not stated, with one sentence sourced to the gazette alone,
   and the amount held in a file no page loads and the build never ships, until a permitted primary text stating it is read
   (section 3 says which). The figure leaves every public surface: the page, its head, its FAQ JSON-LD answer, the home card,
   the README's "verified" table and its Hebrew summary (section 2 and the Folds list every line).
3. **`"verified": true` on a figure config means: every figure a shipped page renders from this file was read in a primary text,
   with the capture and line (or printed page) it was read in, and a dated record of the read.** A dated comparison with search
   results never met that definition; `osek-patur.json:5` has carried the word since `a65a5b2` (7.9.2026) on the strength of
   the 7.9 search alone. This is not a new rule: it is the reading `README.md:64` and `:91` already give the word, applied to the
   one row where it was not.
