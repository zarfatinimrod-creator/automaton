## 1. Creator Rewards Program (CRP)

CRP is the successor to the TikTok Creator Fund (shut in the US in December 2023) and to the
"Creativity Program Beta".

### Eligibility (as reproduced from TikTok's support pages)

| Requirement | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 18+ (19 in South Korea) | 🟢 |
| Account type | Personal account, good standing, authentic info | 🟢 |
| Followers | **≥ 10,000** | 🟢 |
| Views | **≥ 100,000 authentic video views in the previous 30 days** | 🟢 |
| Residence | **an eligible country/region** | 🟢 |
| Video length | **> 1 minute**, posted after acceptance | 🟢 |
| Video reach | ≥ 1,000 qualified For You views to earn | 🟡 |

### The eligible-country list — **ISRAEL IS NOT ON IT**

The list that traces back to TikTok's own support/terms pages is:

> **Brazil, France, Germany, Japan, South Korea, the United Kingdom, the United States** 🟢

Some secondary sources add **Mexico** (🟡) and a few of the worst SEO sources add Australia,
Canada, Spain and Italy (🔴 — these look like the *Series* list bleeding into the CRP list;
do not rely on them).

**No version of the list, in any source I found, includes Israel.** A Hebrew-language source
states it plainly for Israeli readers: in Israel *"האפשרות להרוויח ישירות מצפיות דרך קרן
היוצרים לא קיימת"* — earning directly from views via the creator fund does not exist there
(🟡, boosta.co.il). An English marketing blog aimed at Israelis is blunter: *"Israel has never
been part of TikTok's creator monetization programs… only a 5–10% chance of getting added in
2026"* (🔴 on the probability, 🟡 on the fact — napolify.com).

Some sources claim CRP expansion to Middle East / Southeast Asia markets "expected late 2026"
(🔴 — speculation from an SEO site, no TikTok announcement found). **Do not plan on it.**

### RPM in 2025–2026

TikTok publishes no official RPM. Creator-reported ranges, all secondary:

- $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (🟡)
- $0.40–$1.20 per 1,000 qualified views (🟡)
- $0.20–$0.40 average for US / Western Europe creators (🟡, conflicts with the above)
- One June-2026 sample of disclosed payouts: **~$362–$1,035 per 1,000,000 qualified views,
  average ~$807** (🟡, elev8or.io) — i.e. an effective RPM of ~$0.36–$1.04.

The load-bearing word is **qualified**. Public view counts are filtered by traffic source,
watch duration, authenticity and video eligibility before payment, so monetized views are a
fraction of displayed views (🟢 on the mechanism, 🔴 on any specific ratio).

**Reality check against the mandate:** ₪20,000/month ≈ $5,400/month. At the optimistic
$0.80 RPM that is **~6.75 million qualified views every month, forever**, from one account, in
a country where the program does not operate. This is not a near-miss; it is off by a
geographic gate *and* an order of magnitude.

### Does CRP pay for faceless or AI-assisted content?

Faceless is not banned. **Unoriginal is.** TikTok's Originality Policy and the CRP
video-eligibility rules exclude (🟢 on substance, 🟡 on exact wording):

- "unoriginal content where your account info or video content is copied from others or has
  minimal original input or edits"
- compilations, reposts, lightly-edited re-uploads, **text-to-video slideshows**
- Duets and Stitches
- Photo Mode content
- "low quality content… split screens, meaningless reactions, low-quality images, or slide videos"
- ads, paid promotions, sponsored content

Reported enforcement tightening from **15 September 2025**, with current guidelines effective
**13 September 2025** (🟡). Appeals: reportedly 80 days to appeal a flagged video, ~3 days to
review (🔴).

AI-assisted content is permitted **if labeled** — see §7. But the specific shape a
zero-human-effort pipeline produces (stock/AI b-roll + TTS voice + auto-captions, batch-posted)
sits exactly inside the "minimal original input", "text-to-video slideshow" and "low-quality
images" buckets. **Even relocated to an eligible country, this is the highest-risk content
category on the platform right now.**

**§1 verdict: CLOSED (geography) + CLOSED (content policy).**

## 2. TikTok Shop

### Where it exists

As of **August 2026**, ~**24 local-seller markets** (🟡):

- **Europe (14):** UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria,
  Poland, Czech Republic, Greece, Hungary, Portugal
- **Asia (7):** Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Singapore (+1 unconfirmed)
- **Americas (3):** United States, Mexico, Brazil

Most recent expansion: **15 June 2026** — Austria, Belgium, Netherlands, Poland, plus a
"Sell Across Europe" single-registration tool (🟡; the underlying TikTok newsroom post exists
at `newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-shop-expands-across-europe` but is unreachable from here).

**Israel is not a TikTok Shop market.** No source places it in any list, and no announcement
of a Middle East launch was found. 🟢 (negative finding, consistently supported)

### Could an Israeli sell anyway?

- **US shop:** requires a **US business entity, US bank account, US primary business address
  and a verified tax ID** (🟡). A genuinely-formed US LLC would satisfy this — that is a real
  structure, not a trick — but it drags in US annual compliance (Form 5472 + pro-forma 1120
  for a foreign-owned single-member LLC), a US bank, and inventory/fulfilment. That is
  precisely the "לא רוצה אישורים של עורך דין" territory the mandate rules out, and it is a
  large, ongoing, owner-facing obligation.
- **Cross-Border Trade (CBT):** 2026 rules reported as **business entities only** (individual
  sellers no longer accepted for US/UK CBT), plus a rolling 30-day average of **>50 orders/day**,
  a warehouse in an eligible zip zone, a **$1,500 per-store security deposit**, whitelisted
  logistics, and invitation-only/category-gated direct-ship (🟡). This is a warehouse business,
  not an automation.

### Affiliate programme

Affiliate is **per-market and residency-gated**: "each region's affiliate program is only open
to residents" (🟡). Israel has no market → **no affiliate access**. 🟡→🟢

### Digital products

Even in an open market: *"Virtual or digital products are prohibited unless approved for sale
under the Virtual Goods category"* (🟡). Ebooks, PDFs, templates, design assets, online courses
and video lessons are **not** supported; the Virtual Goods category is **invite-only** and
**code-based** (gift cards, game codes, software licences, prepaid subscriptions, virtual event
tickets). Listing a digital product as if physical is explicitly called out as a violation
risking removal and suspension.

**§2 verdict: CLOSED on all three sub-surfaces.**

## 3. LIVE gifts, diamonds and subscriptions

### Eligibility

Via **Creator Next** (🟢 on substance):

- enrolled in Creator Next
- **live in a region where LIVE Gifts are available**
- **18+** (19 South Korea, 20 Japan)
- account **≥ 30 days old** with **≥ 1,000 followers**
- account in good standing, **not a Business Account**

Some regions reportedly lowered the bar to 500–800 followers (🔴).

### Region

LIVE gifts are reported available in **90+ countries** — far wider than CRP (🟡). Israel
appears in at least one "eligible for TikTok monetization" list alongside Turkey, Greece, Egypt,
Saudi Arabia, UAE etc. (🔴 — that list comes from a low-quality source), and the Hebrew source
above implies Israeli creators' realistic routes are **LIVE gifts + brand deals** (🟡).

**Working assumption: LIVE gifting is probably open to Israel. Confidence 🟡. Not verified.**

### Economics

- Viewer buys Coins → sends Gift → creator receives **Diamonds**
- **1 Diamond ≈ $0.005**; TikTok keeps roughly **50%** of viewer spend (🟡)
- Withdrawal minimum **~$100** (~20,000 diamonds); daily cap **~$1,000**; PayPal or linked
  bank; **~15 business days** to land (🟡)
- LIVE diamond payouts weekly (Wednesdays); subscriptions/Creator Rewards monthly (15th) (🔴)

### Why it is closed to us anyway

TikTok's **LIVE Monetization Guidelines** (🟡, as quoted by third parties):

> "Content that is largely passive, repetitive, unattended, or lacking meaningful viewer
> engagement may be ineligible for monetization."

> "Content with persistently poor or inactive visual presentation, such as prolonged blurred,
> obscured, or static screens that provide little meaningful viewing experience."

Reported explicit bans on leaving a **blank screen, a static image, or a pre-recorded video
looping** for extended periods; and on LIVE with still/animated content over 50% of the screen
and AI/pre-recorded audio.

That is a point-blank description of the only kind of LIVE an unattended bot could run. The
24/7 "AI avatar LIVE" pattern is not a loophole — it is the named violation.

### Subscriptions

- 18+, good standing, **10k followers + 100k views in the past calendar month**, or meet LIVE
  eligibility (🟡)
- Price **$2.99–$99.99/month**, default $5.99 (🟡); one source instead lists fixed brackets
  $0.99 / $1.99 / $2.99 / $4.99 / $9.99 / $19.99 / $49.99 / $99.99 (🔴 — sources conflict)
- Regions reported from September 2024: **UK, Italy, South Korea, France, US** (🟡). **No Israel.**

**§3 verdict: LIVE gifts — region 🟡 open, operationally CLOSED (requires a human host).
Subscriptions — CLOSED.**

## 6. Payouts to Israel

**The honest framing: this section is largely moot, because no first-party surface that pays is
open to an Israeli resident except (probably) LIVE gifts, which we cannot operate.** Recorded
anyway so the colony does not re-research it.

### Rails

- TikTok pays creator earnings to a **linked PayPal account or bank account** via TikTok's
  payment system (🟡).
- **PayPal Israel supports receiving payments and withdrawing to an Israeli bank account** —
  this has been true since PayPal enabled local-bank withdrawals for Israeli accounts (🟢).
  Practical gotcha repeatedly reported: the bank-account holder name **must be in Latin
  characters**, not Hebrew, or PayPal will not accept the link (🟡).

### Thresholds and holds

- Minimum withdrawal **~$100** (LIVE diamonds: ~20,000 diamonds); adjustable in some cases (🟡)
- Daily withdrawal cap **~$1,000**; regional maxima vary (🟡)
- Processing **~15 business days** (🟡)

### Identity and tax documents

- **The owner must personally complete identity verification.** Platform payouts go to
  identified humans. This is exactly the unavoidable, one-time, owner-facing step MISSION.md
  §1 anticipates — it cannot be delegated to an agent. 🟢
- **US tax form:** a non-US person receiving US-source platform income files **Form W-8BEN**
  (individuals) or W-8BEN-E (entities). 🟢
- **Withholding:** without a valid W-8BEN on file, **30% US withholding** applies. Treaty
  relief (Israel and the US have an income-tax treaty) only applies **after** the form is filed,
  and claiming treaty benefits typically requires a **US TIN — an ITIN (Form W-7)** for a
  foreign individual. 🟡 (ITIN requirement is form-line-dependent; verify with an accountant.)

### Does an Israeli individual need a registered עוסק?

**Yes, in substance.** Israeli law requires anyone carrying on income-producing activity to
register: open a **תיק עוסק** at מס הכנסה and register for מע"מ as **עוסק פטור** or **עוסק
מורשה**. Registration as עוסק פטור can now be done **online** without visiting an office
(🟢, per רשות המסים). Content-creator income is ordinary business income — a widely-covered
Israeli case involved a 12-year-old TikToker asking רשות המסים to open an עוסק פטור file
(ynet). 🟢

Two consequences for this mission:

1. **עוסק פטור is not enough at the target.** The עוסק-פטור turnover ceiling is updated
   annually (it has been in the region of ₪120,000/year in recent years — **do not quote this
   number to the owner without re-checking the current-year figure at רשות המסים**). The
   mandate's ₪20,000/month = ₪240,000/year, i.e. **עוסק מורשה** with periodic מע"מ reporting.
2. **This is owner work, and it is unavoidable.** It belongs on the one-time checklist in
   `docs/INCOME_PLAN.he.md` §6 — but there is no point putting it there for TikTok, because
   TikTok has nothing to pay him.

**§6 verdict: rails are fine; there is nothing to send down them.**

## 7. Policy that binds an automated operator

### 7a. Automation — Terms of Service

The rest-of-world Terms prohibit (🟡, as quoted by third parties):

> "use automated scripts to collect information from or otherwise interact with the Services"

The US Terms are narrower but still explicit (🟡):

> "scrape, crawl, export or otherwise extract any data or content in any form, for any purpose,
> from the Platform using any automated system or software, including automated 'bots', except
> as approved in writing by TikTok USDS Joint Venture"

The phrase **"or otherwise interact with"** in the ROW Terms is the operative one for us: it
reaches automated **posting**, liking, following and commenting, not merely scraping. Commonly
enumerated violations: follow/unfollow scripts, auto-likes, mass comments, DM blasters,
scrapers.

**Reading for this colony:** headless-browser or unofficial-API posting to TikTok is a Terms
breach. The **only** sanctioned automation channel is the official Content Posting API (§8) —
which we cannot get. Under constitution rule 4, an unofficial automation path is not a
"technical risk to manage"; it is out of bounds and must not be built.

### 7b. AI-generated content and labels

- AIGC is **allowed, not banned** — the AIGC label is a **disclosure requirement**. 🟢
- Mandatory disclosure for AI-generated visuals/audio depicting **realistic** people or scenes:
  synthetic faces, voice clones, AI backgrounds, photorealistic product images. 🟡
- TikTok reads **C2PA Content Credentials** to auto-detect synthetic media even when the
  creator does not self-disclose; reviewers also flag unlabeled realistic synthetic media. 🟡
- **Exempt:** AI-assisted *text* workflows — scripting, hashtags. 🟡
- Enforcement reported as a 4-tier ladder (warning → 7-day posting restriction → 30-day
  suspension → permanent ban) 🔴 — single low-quality source, treat the ladder as unverified;
  the *existence* of escalating enforcement is 🟢.
- Ads: significantly edited / AI-generated media allowed **only** with the AIGC label or an
  equivalent clear disclaimer/caption/watermark. 🟡

**Reading:** an honest AI pipeline must label. Labeling is not a cost we can dodge, and TikTok
detects unlabeled synthetic media independently of self-disclosure.

### 7c. Unoriginal / repurposed content

Covered in §1. Summary of the binding rule (🟡 wording, 🟢 substance):

> unoriginal content = "copied from others or with minimal original input or edits"

Named triggers: compilations, reposts, lightly-edited re-uploads, **text-to-video slideshows**.
Explicitly *not* sufficient as "creative edit": adding subtitles derived entirely from the
original audio; a basic text summary over another creator's video. Consequence: removal from
the For You feed (i.e. reach collapse) plus ineligibility for rewards. Enforcement tightened
from ~15 Sept 2025, and reportedly applied hardest to the **>1-minute videos CRP is designed to
reward** — the same videos an automated operator would be producing.

### 7d. Multi-account operation

- TikTok publishes **no universal hard cap** on accounts one person may operate; running several
  accounts is a supported feature (the app switcher handles ~3 per device; some sources say up
  to 8). 🟡
- Each account needs a unique email/phone. 🟢
- **What is prohibited is behaviour, not count:** duplicate accounts posting the same video;
  **coordinated inauthentic engagement** (your accounts liking/following/commenting on each
  other); fake engagement; **ban evasion** via new accounts. Those get the whole network
  removed. 🟢

**Reading:** the "spin up 100 faceless accounts and cross-post" pattern — the obvious way an
agent colony would try to brute-force TikTok — is the textbook description of what gets
network-banned. It is also dishonest volume rather than honest value, so it fails
`constitution.md` before it fails TikTok's rules.

## 8. The Content Posting API

### Who can get access

**The blocking fact:** TikTok's developer onboarding states it *"is currently unable to onboard
personal accounts or individual developers"* — the registration expects a **company**, with a
**company website** (not a personal one), and a declared **Primary Developer Location** (the
development team's primary location). 🟡

An Israeli individual with no company therefore cannot even register a client, let alone pass
the audit.

### Scopes and the unaudited ceiling

- `video.upload` — pushes a video to the creator's **inbox/drafts**; a human then finishes and
  publishes it in the app.
- `video.publish` — **Direct Post**, publishes straight to the profile.

For **unaudited** clients (🟡, consistently reported):

- all content posted through the endpoint is **restricted to `SELF_ONLY` (private) viewership**
- **at most 5 users** may post through the client in a rolling **24-hour** window
- **every user account posting through the client must be set to private at the time of posting**

So an unaudited client cannot make anything public. That kills the automated-posting idea
outright at the free tier.

### What the audit demands

A **second, separate review** beyond ordinary app approval (🟡): a **recorded demo of the full
posting flow**, a **privacy policy URL** (and terms), and **evidence the integration lives
inside a finished, real product**. US/UK/EU launch additionally requires company ownership,
personnel, service/support-location details; affiliation with restricted countries limits
access to sensitive data. 🟡

### What the terms permit

TikTok's **Content Sharing Guidelines** wrap the API in UX obligations: the creator must review
and confirm the post, commercial content must be disclosed, TikTok's policies must be surfaced
in the flow. 🟡 The API is architected around **a human pressing publish**. "The owner does
nothing" is not merely hard to build here — it is contrary to the integration's design intent.

**§8 verdict: CLOSED. No company → no client. No audit → private-only. Audit passed → still a
human-in-the-loop publishing UX.**

## What this means for a zero-human-effort operator

1. **TikTok is not a revenue line for this colony. Recommend KILL / do-not-start.**
   Not "hard", not "slow" — structurally closed. Israel is absent from the Creator Rewards
   country list, absent from TikTok Shop's ~24 markets, absent from Series, absent from
   Subscriptions, absent from Effect Creator Rewards. The two surfaces that might reach an
   Israeli — LIVE gifts and possibly Creator Marketplace — require the exact two things the
   owner ruled out: **being on camera**, and **talking to people**.

2. **Even in an eligible country, the content shape is the problem.** The 2025–2026 originality
   crackdown targets compilations, reposts, light re-edits and text-to-video slideshows — the
   native output of a fully automated pipeline. Buying our way into a US-facing setup would move
   the wall, not remove it.

3. **Do not try the workarounds, and do not let a future session rediscover them as "ideas".**
   Explicitly out of bounds under `constitution.md` §4 and TikTok's terms:
   - VPN / foreign SIM / region spoofing to fake residency for a residency-keyed programme —
     TikTok fingerprints devices and behaviour, and reported consequences include permanent ban
     **and withheld funds**;
   - buying or renting an aged foreign account;
   - unofficial automation (headless-browser posting, follow/like/comment bots);
   - a farm of cross-posting accounts with mutual engagement;
   - listing digital products as physical goods on TikTok Shop;
   - publishing realistic AI content without the AIGC label.
   A US LLC formed **genuinely** to run a real US TikTok Shop business is *not* in this list —
   it is legitimate — but it is a heavy, owner-facing, accountant-and-warehouse business, which
   is the opposite of the mandate.

4. **The only non-forbidden TikTok role is unpaid top-of-funnel**, and it is weak: original,
   AIGC-labeled, >1-minute videos driving traffic to a product we own and bill for elsewhere
   (where Israeli payment rails and our own terms apply). Note the cost side is still real —
   posting must be manual or via an approved API belonging to a company, and content must be
   genuinely original, which is the expensive part. **Judge this against other channels on its
   traffic value alone; assume TikTok itself pays ₪0.**

5. **What would change the verdict** (worth a cheap quarterly re-check, nothing more):
